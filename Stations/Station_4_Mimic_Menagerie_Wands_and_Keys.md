# Station 4: The Mimic Menagerie — Wands and Keys

> **Visibility key:** sections marked **[DM ONLY]** stay behind the screen. Only the items marked **[PLAYER HANDOUT]** or **[PLAYER-FACING PROP]** go on the table.

## Source & Metadata  [DM ONLY]
*   **Source:** *Wands and Keys*, *The Game Master's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons* (Jeff Ashworth), Ch 11 Gateway Puzzles, pp. 122–141 (exact page not recorded). Replaces the generic chest-guessing room.
    *   **Book mechanics (unscaled):** four wands (poison, 1 charge; acid, 1 charge; mage hand; bright light), all working within 30 ft. Alcove 1 illusion key (shadow doesn't change, Perception 15); Alcove 2 adolescent mimic (revealed by poison; +7 to hit, 11 (2d8+2) + 4 acid); Alcove 3 explosive construct (revealed by acid; DC 15 Dex, 4 + 4 damage); Alcove 4 real key (mage hand moves it). Opening the wrong alcove resets the room and shuffles the keys.
*   **Alternate:** *The Doors Without Traps* (Ch 11).
*   **Estimated Playtime:** 10–12 minutes (Simulated Playtest: **10m 50s**, inside the 10–15 min target, with one reset. See the [Station 4 Playtest report](Playtests/Station_4_Playtest.md)).
*   **Type:** Tool-based deduction, sensory investigation, and ranged hazard containment.
*   **Players:** Scooby-Doo (Barbarian 5) & Murray Mummy (Druid 5).
*   **Goal:** Use four diagnostic wands to identify the hazards in four alcoves and safely retrieve **Crest #4** without setting off the mimic or the explosive construct.

## DM Notes (Coaching)  [DM ONLY]

### What this room is testing
This is a "test before you touch" room. Four keys look identical; three are hazards. The players have four tools, but **two of them (Poison and Acid) only fire once per reset**, so they can't just zap everything. The fun is in noticing which tool answers which question ("is it fake?", "is it alive?", "is it a machine?", "how do I pick it up without touching it?"), spending the one-shot wands wisely, and using Scooby's nose to close the gap. Success is a *plan*, not a lucky roll. The one way to lose time is grabbing a key by hand (or by mage hand) before they know what it is.

### Describing the room neutrally (read-aloud)
> *"The door seals behind you. This round chamber smells of dust and old lamp oil. Four stone arches are set into the far wall, numbered I to IV in tarnished brass. In each one, a silver key with the Von Zarovich wolf crest sits on a stone pedestal. They look exactly alike. Twenty feet back from the arches stands a stone table with four wands resting in carved cradles. A line of brass studs is set into the floor between the table and the arches."*

*   Describe all four alcoves with the **same words**. If a player asks "which one looks different?", the honest answer is "to the naked eye, none of them".
*   Never describe a key's position, shine, temperature, or smell unless a test or check earned it.
*   Let players pick up the wand cards right away. Do **not** read the plaque (Clue 3) at the start; it is under dust on the back of the wand rack.

### Narrating each wand the same way every time
Use the **Wand Reaction Matrix** below. The rule of thumb: a wand only produces a *dramatic* effect on the one thing it is "for". On everything else, give a short, boring result. Boring results are information too, so say them with the same tone every time. Never add flavour to the real key that you wouldn't add to the mimic (the Acid result on those two must sound identical).

### Nudge lines (say aloud, never the answer)
*   **Stuck at the start / about to grab a key:** "Before you reach in, does anything about this room tell you what happened to the last people here?" (points to Clue 1)
*   **Using the wands at random:** "What do you think each wand is *for*? What question does it answer?"
*   **Forgot the charges:** "Take a look at the wand cards. Anything printed on them you haven't used yet?"
*   **Light wand with no plan:** "When you sweep the beam, what are you watching?"
*   **Ignoring Scooby:** "Scooby, your stomach just growled. Anything on your sheet that a snack helps with?" (Clue 2)
*   **Two keys left, no wands left:** "Is there anything either of you can do that doesn't use a wand?"
*   **Debating forever:** "You've got one more minute before the torches gutter. What's your plan?"

### What NOT to say or do
*   Don't say which alcove holds anything, even as a joke ("definitely not Alcove IV, heh").
*   Don't react with your face when they point at the real key. Keep the same "Okay, what do you do?" for every alcove.
*   Don't put the mimic standee, construct card, or Crest token on the table until the matching reveal happens.
*   Don't call the wands "the mimic wand" or "the bomb wand". Use their names: Bright Light, Poison, Acid, Mage Hand.
*   Don't punish testing. Zapping a wand from behind the stud line is always safe.
*   Don't leave the Answer Key / Shuffle Tracker face-up. **DM eyes only.** Keep it on a clipboard or behind the screen.

### Pacing checkpoints
| Clock | Check | Do this |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **00:00** | Read-aloud, place 4 alcove cards and 4 wand cards. Set the Shuffle Tracker to this pair's starting layout. | Start the timer. |
| **02:00–03:00** | No wand used yet, or someone is reaching for a key? | Give **Clue 1** (scorch marks and studs). |
| **05:00–06:00** | They've started testing (or argued for a while) but haven't used Scooby. | Give **Clue 2** (Snack Surge prompt; toss a real snack). |
| **08:00–09:00** | Still unsure which wand does what, or about to waste a charge. | Reveal **Clue 3** (the dusty plaque). |
| **11:00** | Stuck, or just suffered a reset. | **Clue 4** bailout (Murray's bandage tap). |
| **13:00** | Not solved. | The real key starts to glow faintly ("the crest knows it's wanted"). Solve by 14:00. |
| **15:00** | Hard stop. | Hand over Crest #4 with a funny cost (Scooby's tail is singed, Murray's wraps are tangled). Move on. |

A reset (wrong alcove) costs about 45 seconds of table time. If a reset happens after 10:00, go straight to Clue 4.

### Handling derails
*   **Grabbing a key immediately:** Let it happen. Resolve it from the Wand Reaction Matrix ("Grab by hand" row), reset, shuffle, and recharge the wands. The second attempt is usually much sharper. If it's the first 60 seconds, you may give a single warning first: "You reach in... are you sure? The floor in front of you is scorched."
*   **Attacking everything:** Attacks count as disturbing that alcove. Hitting the mimic wakes it (combat-lite, then reset). Hitting the construct detonates it (then reset). Hitting the illusion passes through it (reveal, no reset). Hitting the real key clangs off harmlessly (it's warded), which tells them nothing on its own. Murray's **Sand Blast** cone hits up to 3 adjacent alcoves at once: resolve each one, and don't soften the chaos. It's a fun lesson.
*   **Clever alternatives (the book says reward them):**
    *   *Bandage Whip pull (Murray, reach 2 squares):* lifting a key with the whip counts the same as Mage Hand. It works on the real key; on a hazard it triggers that hazard.
    *   *Tapping the pedestal base with a whip or a pole (without lifting the key):* safe. Give that one alcove's tap sound from Clue 4. Each tap takes an action. This is the bailout clue, earned early, so allow it.
    *   *Throwing an object to knock a key off:* counts as lifting (triggers a hazard; wins on the real key).
    *   *Sniffing without a snack:* Scooby's normal Perception (+4) vs **DC 15** for "something alive" or "something fake".
    *   *Testing a wand on themselves or each other:* Poison: DC 10 Con save or 1 damage and a coughing fit (and the charge is spent). Acid: 1 damage and a smoking eyebrow (charge spent). Light and Mage Hand: harmless and silly.
    *   *Anything else plausible:* if it answers one question about one alcove at a cost (an action, a charge, a risk), say yes.

### Adjudicating the character hooks
*   **Scooby — Scooby Snack Surge** (sheet: *Action: eat a snack to instantly gain +10 to Perception to sniff out traps/mimics*; Scooby's Perception is +4, so +14 total). Requires an actual snack (toss a real one). Scooby must ask **one** question: "something alive?" (finds the mimic) or "something fake?" (finds the illusion). Roll vs DC 12; on +14 he only fails on a natural 1 or 2. One use per short rest. **After a reset**, Scooby can sniff again only without the surge (DC 15) unless you hand over a second snack as a mercy.
*   **Murray — Sandstorm Shuffle** (sheet: *Action: unravel bandages to automatically grapple and restrain an enemy*; the overview says 2 rounds). There is no initiative here: when the mimic springs, it gets **one** bite at whoever disturbed it (or it chomps the mage hand and gets no bite), then Murray acts. Shuffle automatically restrains it for 2 rounds, and Scooby's two Big Bites (+7, 1d8+4 each) normally finish its 15 HP.
*   **Murray — Nature (+4):** if Murray asks what he knows about mimics, Nature DC 10: "Mimics digest with acid. Acid won't bother one, but poison makes them gag." This is a fair alternate to Clue 2.
*   **Murray — Bandage Whip (reach 2 squares):** used for the whip pull and the Clue 4 tap (see above).

## Room Setup  [DM ONLY: full detail; read-aloud is above]
*   Four arched stone alcoves (**Alcove I, II, III, IV**) each hold what appears to be a gleaming silver Von Zarovich Crest Key on a pedestal. All four look identical.
*   A stone table 20 feet away holds four magical testing wands (work up to 30 ft). A line of brass studs marks the 30-ft line in front of the arches.
    1.  **Wand of Bright Light** (casts a concentrated beam of daylight; unlimited uses).
    2.  **Wand of Poison** (puffs a 30-ft jet of purple contact toxin; **1 charge**).
    3.  **Wand of Acid** (fires a squirt of green corrosive solvent; **1 charge**).
    4.  **Wand of Mage Hand** (creates an invisible telekinetic hand to grip objects; unlimited uses).
*   **Reset:** taking (lifting) the key from any wrong alcove, by hand, mage hand, whip, or thrown object, triggers that alcove and then resets the room: the arches grind and rotate, the contents shuffle (advance the Shuffle Tracker), and both one-charge wands refill.

### Wand Reaction Matrix  [DM ONLY]: read the same words every time
| Wand → / Content ↓ | Bright Light | Poison (1 charge) | Acid (1 charge) | Mage Hand (lifts) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Illusion** | Shadow stays frozen. **Reveal.** | "The mist drifts straight *through* the key." **Reveal.** | "The acid passes through and hisses on the wall behind." **Reveal.** | Gong, vapour. **Reset.** |
| **Mimic** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | Gags and slumps. **Reveal.** | "The acid splashes over the key and hisses on the pedestal. The key looks unharmed." | Bites the hand, lunges. **Reset.** |
| **Construct** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | "The mist beads on the metal and drifts away." | Casing melts, fuse dies. **Reveal (disarmed).** | Explodes. **Reset.** |
| **Real key** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | "The mist beads on the metal and drifts away." | "The acid splashes over the key and hisses on the pedestal. The key looks unharmed." | **Success.** |

## Failure & Combat Scaling (For 14-Year-Olds)  [DM ONLY]
*   **Triggering the Mimic:** Book stats are +7 to hit, 11 (2d8+2) + 4 acid; scaled here to **+5 to hit, 6 (1d8+2) piercing + 2 acid**, AC 12, 15 HP. No initiative: one bite, then **Murray's Sandstorm Shuffle** (Action) automatically restrains it for 2 rounds, and Scooby's two Big Bites (+7, 1d8+4) or Murray's Bandage Whip (+5, 1d6+2) finish it in about a round. Then reset.
*   **Triggering the Construct:** Book is DC 15 Dex, 4 + 4 damage; scaled here to **DC 12 Dex, 5 (2d4), half on a save**, for everyone within 30 ft. Then reset.
*   **Triggering the Illusion:** no damage, just a gong and a reset.
*   Scooby has 55 HP and Murray 38, so neither hazard can knock anyone out. The real cost is time and the shuffle.

## The 4 Progressive Clues & Handouts  [DM ONLY: clue text; deliver aloud or as a card when triggered]

### Clue 1: The Scorched Floor (Sensory / Environmental), minute 2–3
*   **Trigger:** Players look around the room, ask what happened here, or someone reaches for a key with no plan.
*   **Text (read aloud):**
    > *"Just past the line of brass studs, the floor is scorched in a black fan shape. There are deep tooth gouges in the flagstones and a dried, sticky puddle. Nothing like that is on your side of the line. The wand cradles are worn smooth, as if people have stood at this table many times. Two of the cradles have a single empty socket where a charge-gem once sat. There's a single gem still in each."*
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** Tells them grabbing by hand goes badly, that the stud line is the safe zone, and that two wands are limited. It doesn't say which alcove holds what (the marks are in front of all four, because the contents shuffle).

### Clue 2: Scooby Snack Surge (Character Sheet Feature), minute 5–6
*   **Trigger:** Toss Scooby a real treat! Scooby uses *Scooby Snack Surge* (Action; +10 Perception, total +14, DC 12) and asks **one** question: "alive?" or "fake?"
*   **Text (fill in from the current layout):**
    > *"Scooby's snout twitches into overdrive. Alcove ___ smells like a wet swamp monster that just ate a meatloaf. Something in there is breathing."* (alive → mimic)
    > *or:* *"Alcove ___ smells of... nothing at all. Not metal, not dust. It's like the key isn't there."* (fake → illusion)
*   **Alternate:** Murray's Nature DC 10 mimic lore (see DM Notes).
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** It rewards using a sheet feature and removes one hazard, but never points at the real key.

### Clue 3: The Menagerie Plaque (Logic / Formula Constraint), minute 8–9
*   **Trigger:** Investigation DC 10 on the wand rack, or the DM reveals it at 8:00–9:00 ("as you set a wand down, you smear the dust off a brass plaque on the back of the rack").
*   **Text/Handout [PLAYER HANDOUT]:**
    > *"Four keys wait, and three are lies.*
    > *What casts no true shadow, the sun will expose;*
    > *What lives will choke where the venom blows;*
    > *What ticks, the acid melts from within;*
    > *One breath, one drop: spend them, then win.*
    > *Touch none with your hand. Let the ghost hand begin."*
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** It states the rules (wand → hazard, one charge each, retrieve with Mage Hand) but not the locations.

### Clue 4: Murray's Bandage Tap (Fail-Forward Bailout), reserve only
*   **Trigger:** Strictly in reserve. Use it only after a critical mistake (a reset after 10:00) or at **11:00** if they're stuck. Prompt: "Murray, your Bandage Whip reaches 2 squares. Want to give the pedestals a gentle tap, without touching the keys?"
*   **Text (fill in from the current layout):**
    > *"Tap... the pedestal under Alcove ___ lets out an irritated, wet snore. Tap... Alcove ___ gives off a faint clockwork tick-tick-tick. Tap... the whip passes straight through the key in Alcove ___. Tap... Alcove ___ rings like a pure, hollow brass bell."*
*   **Calibration:** **Too Helpful (by design): safety net.** It gives the whole answer. Only use it to protect the session clock.

### 1. The Four Alcove Cards / Display Mats  [PLAYER-FACING PROP]
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 5" x 7" vertical cards or tabletop display placemats.
*   **Art Style:** Dark gothic dungeon alcoves lit by eerie purple witch-light torches, with carved stone pedestals.
*   **All four cards use the SAME artwork:** a stone alcove holding a silver wolf-crest key on a plain stone pedestal, with an ordinary torch-cast shadow. *   **Dimensions/Format:** 2.5" x 7" bookmark-sized wand cards or 3D prop tags.
*   **Art Style:** Ornate wizarding wands carved from exotic magical woods with glowing crystal foci. Print **"RANGE 30 FT (6 squares)"** on each card.

## DM Tips & Running Recommendations  [DM ONLY]
1.  **Rotate starting layouts:** use the Shuffle Tracker and start each pair on a different layout so spoilers don't travel between groups.
2.  **Make the shuffle physical but secret:** on a reset, read: *"With a grinding roar, the four arches spin like a roulette wheel and lock back into place. Whatever you learned about them, it's all scrambled now. Two fresh gems click into the wand cradles."* Then sweep all reveal tiles back into your box, return the charge beads, and move the paperclip. The alcove cards themselves never move (they're identical anyway). Saying out loud that "everything is scrambled" stops players arguing about whether old reveals still count.
3.  **Enforce the charge bead:** the moment a player says they fire Poison or Acid, hold out your hand for the bead *before* you narrate the result. That makes the one-charge limit physical, so impulsive players feel the cost.
4.  **Fast-Track Mimic Combat:** if the mimic attacks, don't roll initiative. Give it one bite, then let Murray use *Sandstorm Shuffle* (his Action) and let Scooby take his two Big Bites (1d8+4 each) to finish it.
5.  **Say the boring results with conviction:** "Its shadow swings with the beam" is a clue. Say it the same way for every normal key.
6.  **Early finish:** if a pair solves it before 8:00, offer a bonus: "There's one more charge-gem rattling in the rack. Can you tell me which alcove was which?" A correct full answer earns a treat or inspiration for the next station.
