# Station 2 Playtest Report: The Alchemist's Laboratory (The Trickster's Coin)

Simulated mock playtest of [Station_2_Alchemist_Lab_Tricksters_Coin.md](../Station_2_Alchemist_Lab_Tricksters_Coin.md). This is a simulation, not a live session: player behaviour is modelled on typical 12–16-year-old tables. All dice were rolled for real before the narrative was written and are reported as rolled (pre-rolled sequence: d20s 12, 2, 8, 20, 15, 11, 12, 16, 19, 14, 10, 14; d6s 4, 4, 6, 4; used in order, unused rolls discarded).

Two runs were played:
*   **Run A**: the corrected station with the book's fixed answer (Vial #6 is always the fake). It exposed a design problem (solved by luck, under target).
*   **Run B**: the same pair profile on a fresh table, after the **Trickster's Shuffle** rule was added (see *Changes Applied*). Run B is the result reported in the station metadata.

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Playtest Transcript

### Run A (fixed answer #6), condensed

*   **00:00** DM reads the room aloud and lays out the rack of 10 vial cards, the scale mat, the jester standee and a blank Weighing Log. Jayden: "Ten potions? It's a Skyrim alchemy table. I'm drinking number one." He grabs the card.
*   **00:40** DM: the seal won't break, a drop leaks onto his tongue. Side effect **d6 = 4: Sneezing Fit**. Jayden fake-sneezes the Weighing Log off the table. Both laugh. Iron Stomach means no damage.
*   **01:30** Owen pokes the ingredient shelf ("could be eye of newt for a recipe?"). It's a red herring, about 1 minute lost.
*   **02:30** DM entry nudge ("the jester's bowl looks like it's waiting for something"). Owen inspects the altar, gets **Handout 1**, and rolls Religion **d20 = 12 + 4 = 16, success**: the god of forgers wants the false thing.
*   **03:30** Short argument: "lightest craft" means a boat? Owen: "No, the lightest *vial*. The scale is right there."
*   **04:10** Jayden slaps #1 and #2 on the pans. **W1: 1 v 2, LEVEL.** Jayden: "Useless!" Owen: "No, it means neither is fake."
*   **05:20** DM prompts Esmeralda. Owen uses her. She clears **#1**, which they already knew was fine, so the clue was wasted ("Thanks, rat").
*   **06:00** **W2: 3 v 4, LEVEL.**
*   **06:45** **W3: 5 v 6, RIGHT RISES.** Jayden: "SIX! It's six!" Owen: "...yeah, the rising side is lighter."
*   **07:20** Owen double-checks the inscription. Jayden places #6 in front of the jester.
*   **07:40** The door opens; Crest #2 awarded. High-five.

**Finding:** Pair weighing in numerical order is what most teens try first. It hits #6 on the third weighing every time, so the station is too short and skips its whole teaching point. The ledger (Clue 3) was never reached. This is a real design problem, not just a lucky table. See *Changes Applied*.

### Run B (with Trickster's Shuffle), full transcript
*DM keeps the Suspect Tracker (10 boxes) behind the screen. Suspects listed in brackets are DM-only.*

#### 00:00–03:00: Entry, early theories, first prop inspection
*   **00:00** DM reads the room aloud. Props on the table: rack of 10 identical vial cards (2" x 3"), scale mat, jester standee, blank Weighing Log, pencil. [Suspects: 1–10]
*   **00:20** Jayden: "Bro, it's a potion shop. Minecraft rules: drink everything." He picks up vial card #4 and mimes chugging.
*   **00:35** DM: enchanted seal, one drop on the tongue. Side effect **d6 = 4: Sneezing Fit (again)**. Jayden: "*Every* time!" He sneezes the tilt arrow off the mat; Owen puts it back. No damage (Iron Stomach).
*   **01:10** Owen holds two cards up to the light: "Does one look different? Different liquid line?" DM: "They look exactly the same." Owen nods. (The identical card art worked: no visual shortcut.)
*   **01:30** Distraction: both search the ingredient shelf. Jayden insists the "Powdered Bat Wing" jar must go in the bowl. About 1 minute of arguing.
*   **02:30** **Clue 1.** DM entry nudge. Owen reads **Handout 1** aloud. Religion: Owen **d20 = 2 + 4 = 6, fail**. Jayden tries: **d20 = 8 + 0 = 8, fail**. Jayden groans: "We're both dumb, cool." They keep the handout but don't get the god-of-forgers lore.

#### 03:00–07:00: First hypothesis, clue, first failure and reaction
*   **03:00** Semantics argument: Jayden says "hollow lie" means an *empty* vial and starts shaking cards near his ear. Owen: "They're cards, dude." Owen points to "lightest craft" and the scale. They agree they want the lightest vial. (About 1 minute of debate.)
*   **03:50** **W1: 1 v 2.** DM checks the Tracker (left 1, right 1, off-scale 8; biggest group is off-scale): **"The pans hang perfectly level."** Tilt card on LEVEL. Owen logs it. [Suspects: 3–10]
*   **04:30** Jayden, impatient: "This'll take a year." He dumps 3, 4, 5 on the left and 6, 7 on the right. **W2 (uneven):** "The LEFT pan drops... the RIGHT pan rises." Jayden: "Right side's lighter, so it's six or seven!" DM nudge: *"How many vials are on each pan right now?"* Owen: "Three versus two. Of course the three side is heavier." Jayden: "...ugh." No information. [Suspects unchanged: 3–10]
*   **05:30** **Clue 2.** DM: *"Quasimodo, your rat's whiskers are twitching at the rack."* Owen scans his sheet and finds **Esmeralda the Rat** ("identify a poison"). He uses his action. #1 is already cleared, so Esmeralda clears the lowest suspect, **#3** (cracked seal, real quicksilver). Owen slides #3 off to the side. [Suspects: 4–10]
*   **06:10** **First critical failure.** While Owen is writing in the log, Jayden grabs **#6** ("it rose, I'm calling it") and drops it at the jester. Six suspects remain, so it's wrong. Sizzle, pink smoke, and the jester spits it back. Side effect **d6 = 6: Tingling Fingers**. From now on Jayden must move cards with two fingers of each hand, which slows his card handling and gets a big laugh. Owen: "*That's* why I said wait." [Suspects: 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10]
*   **06:30** DM decision: a critical mistake makes Clue 4 *available*, but the table isn't stuck and is laughing, so the DM holds it. (Correct per the trigger rule.)

#### 07:00–11:00: Deep deduction, ability activation, debate
*   **07:00** Owen: "OK, pairs. Four versus five." **W3: 4 v 5** (left 1, right 1, off-scale 4) → **LEVEL.** Owen crosses off 4 and 5 on the log. [Suspects: 7, 8, 9, 10]
*   **07:45** Owen counts the log: "That's three uses." He notices the crack icon on row 4. "Fourth one creaks. We can't keep doing pairs forever." Jayden: "Speedrun strats, bro, there's gotta be a skip."
*   **08:15** **Clue 3.** DM: *"That writing desk hasn't been touched yet."* Owen: Investigation **d20 = 20 + 4 = 24**. Natural 20, and both players cheer. He reads **Handout 2** aloud.
*   **08:50** **Aha moment.** Owen re-reads "when it hangs even, it speaks of the bottles left on the table." "Wait, when it was level, the fake was in the ones we didn't weigh. So we can weigh some and leave some aside and still learn something either way." Jayden: "So it's like a three-way door, not two." They move the four suspects to the mat's **Off the Scale** tray to see them clearly.
*   **09:30** Debate (about 1 minute): Jayden wants 2 v 2. Owen: "If it's 2 v 2, it tilts, and we still have two left. If we do 1 v 1 and keep 2 aside, we might get it right away." (Both need 2 weighings in the worst case, so either is fine. The DM stays quiet.) They go with Owen's plan.
*   **10:20** **W4: 7 v 8**, with 9 and 10 aside (left 1, right 1, off-scale 2) → **LEVEL.** The bat fulcrum **creaks** (4th weighing warning). Jayden: "Boss music." [Suspects: 9, 10]
*   **10:50** Jayden reaches for #9 to offer it. Owen blocks: "Fifty-fifty, and if you're wrong you get another curse." DM nudge: *"Do you have a weighing left? Is guessing worth the risk?"*

#### 11:00–15:00: Solution, door unlocks, celebration
*   **11:00** Checkpoint: the table isn't stuck (2 suspects, clear plan), so **Clue 4 is not deployed.**
*   **11:05** **W5: 9 v 10.** The bat **shrieks** (past the cap). The two suspects tie, so the DM picks: **"The RIGHT pan rises."** Owen, who weighed, rolls the side effect: **d6 = 4: Sneezing Fit.** "Now *I* have it?!" [Suspects: 10]
*   **11:15** Owen (sneezing) places **#10** in front of the jester. Jayden pats the card in with two fingers of each hand.
*   **11:20** **SOLVED.** The jester's grin cracks wide, the iron door swings open, and **Crest #2** is handed over. Big double high-five, and Jayden does a victory sneeze. **Stopwatch: 11:20.**

## 4. Physical Prop Usability Report

*   **Vial cards (10):** Identical art worked. Owen tried a visual comparison at 01:10 and found no tell, which is the intended result (the old "lighter meniscus" on #6 would have let him solve it by looking). Large Arabic numerals were legible across the table. Underlining the 6 and 9 mattered: Jayden flipped #6 upside down once while juggling cards.
*   **Token count:** 10 vial cards plus a tilt arrow plus 6 penalty cards was manageable. With five 2" x 3" cards per pan the mat gets crowded; the cards must overlap. Not a problem in Run B (they never did 5 v 5), but a 5 v 5 table will need the larger pan circles in the spec.
*   **Scale mat layout:** The tilt card in the LEFT RISES / LEVEL / RIGHT RISES boxes plus the fixed phrasing kept results unambiguous. The **Off the Scale** tray was the most useful prop after the ledger: it made the third group visible. Recommend placing set-aside cards there every time.
*   **Weighing Log:** Owen used it constantly, and the crack and skull icons on rows 4–5 made him change strategy at 07:45. Jayden ignored it. Give it to the analytical player.
*   **Penalty cards:** Tingling Fingers had a physical effect (moving cards with two fingers of each hand). It was funny, slowed Jayden by maybe 20 seconds total, and was not frustrating.
*   **DM Suspect Tracker:** Cost roughly 5–10 seconds per weighing. One near-slip at W3: the DM almost forgot #6 had been cleared by the wrong offering. Recommend crossing off **before** speaking.
*   **Confusion points:** (1) the uneven-pan weighing at 04:30, resolved by the nudge in about 30 s; (2) "hollow lie" read as "empty vial", resolved by the players in about 1 min.

## 5. DM Tuning Recommendations

### Pacing adjustments for live play
*   **Use the Trickster's Shuffle.** Without it, the most common first strategy solves the room in about 7 minutes by luck.
*   **Prompt Esmeralda earlier (around 4:00) if the players are weighing pairs.** That way she clears a fresh suspect instead of one they've already weighed.
*   **Uneven-pan weighings:** they count toward the cap. If the table is running long, waive the first one with the nudge line.
*   **Expect an analytical/impulsive split.** The impulsive player will offer a vial early (06:10 here). That's fine: the side effect is entertainment. Don't rush Clue 4 just because of it.
*   **Optimal play is fast:** a table that finds the 3-3-3 split right after Esmeralda can finish in about 7–8 minutes even under the Shuffle. That's acceptable (they earned it). If you want to stretch a fast table, add the Room 2 flavour (the Trickster's laugh, a creaking floor) rather than more puzzle.

### Emergency time-savers (if the group runs slow)
1.  Skip the ledger's Investigation roll and hand Clue 3 straight over at 8:00.
2.  Drop the shriek penalty for weighings 5+ (keep the creak warning only).
3.  Deploy Clue 4 at 10:00 instead of 11:00 if the whole session is behind schedule.
4.  At 14:00, hard stop: the Trickster gets bored and opens the door. Award Crest #2 anyway. Never let one station eat the hour.

## Changes Applied (to the station file, after Run A)
1.  **Added the Trickster's Shuffle rule (recommended mode).** The DM keeps a Suspect Tracker, and the fake "hides in the biggest group" of suspects on each even weighing. Every ruling stays consistent with a real fake, but lucky pair weighing no longer wins on the third try. Good group weighing still solves in 3 weighings (10 suspects) or 2 (9 suspects, 3-3-3 split). The fixed #6 answer is kept as the book-default option and is required for real-weight props.
2.  **Esmeralda fallback:** she clears #1, or the lowest-numbered remaining suspect if #1 is already cleared, and only while 3 or more suspects remain.
3.  **Suspect Tracker** added to the props as a DM-only item.
4.  **Clue 4 clarified:** under the Shuffle, the three bubble-marked vials become the only suspects.
5.  Metadata playtime and Playtest Notes updated to point at this report (the stale 13m 30s "Marcus/Tyler" numbers were removed).

Pre-playtest fixes (Task 1) are listed in the station file's structure: rewritten all-branch solution trees, the corrected 9-suspect claim, the ledger rewritten as a constraint, the strict Clue 4 trigger, identical vial art, the at-table scale rules, the desk added to the room, and the Station 4 alternate conflict noted.
