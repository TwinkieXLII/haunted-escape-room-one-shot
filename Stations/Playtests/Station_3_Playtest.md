# Station 3 Playtest Report: The Clockwork Observatory (Of Gears and Gadgets)

*Simulated mock playtest of the redesigned station (constellation riddle + star chart + crank-check version). Station file: [../Station_3_Clockwork_Observatory_Gears_and_Gadgets.md](../Station_3_Clockwork_Observatory_Gears_and_Gadgets.md).*

---

## 1. Simulation Metadata
*   **Station:** Station 3, The Clockwork Observatory: *Of Gears and Gadgets* (Ashworth, Ch 12)
*   **Character pair:** Count Dracula / Drac (Bard 5: AC 14, 38 HP) & Johnny (Bard 5: AC 13, 38 HP)
*   **Players:**
    *   **Player 1 — "Tyler," age 13, playing Johnny.** Enthusiastic, impatient, impulsive, jokey.
    *   **Player 2 — "Owen," age 15, playing Drac.** Analytical, reads his character sheet, reins Tyler in.
*   **Props on table:** door board (11" x 8.5"), 7 gear tokens (sim used the *old* size spec, where gear size grew with tooth count, 2.5"–4"), plaque card, star chart (8" print), phantom standee, phone timer.
*   **Dice:** all rolls below come from one honest random sequence, used in order: d20 = 12, 7, 1, 13, 5, 8, 5; d6 = 2, 1.
*   **Total stopwatch solve time: 11m 20s.** Target is 10–15 min: **within target.** Clue 4 was not needed.

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Playtest Transcript

### 00:00 – 03:00 · Entry, first theories, first prop inspection
*   **00:00** The DM reads the room aloud and places the door board, the jumbled gear pile and the phantom standee. Tyler immediately picks up the standee: *"Bro, it's a Dark Souls boss. Can I hit it?"* Owen: *"Read the door first."*
*   **00:30** Owen asks to look at the door, and the DM hands over the **plaque card**. Owen reads it aloud. Tyler: *"'Great WORM'? We're fighting a worm?"* Owen: *"It's spelled w-y-r-m. That's dragon. Like in Skyrim."* Tyler: *"Oh, wyrm. Okay, yeah, dragon."* (The first decode takes 20 seconds.)
*   **01:10** Tyler sorts the gears by size into a line on the mat: *"These are like Mario Kart cups, small to big."* He counts teeth out loud and loses count on the two 7s, then recounts.
*   **01:40** *Distraction:* Tyler says the riddle's "lights" must mean candles and asks if there's a candle shelf. The DM says there's a bookshelf of astronomy tomes; Tyler searches it for about 45 seconds and finds nothing useful. Owen keeps re-reading "first wing."
*   **02:30** They still haven't touched the astrolabe, so the DM delivers **Clue 1 (The Painted Dome)**. Owen: *"Golden line to Peg 1... the astrolabe!"* The DM hands over the **star chart**. Both lean over it; Tyler's elbow covers the Wolf. The 8" print is a little small for two heads.
*   **02:50** Owen lays the plaque next to the chart and starts pointing: *"Dragon is right there."*

### 03:00 – 07:00 · First hypothesis, clue, first failure
*   **03:00 · Phantom tick #1 → Johnny.** Attack d20 **12** + 5 = **17** vs AC 13: **hit**. Damage 1d6 (**2**) + 2 = **4** (Johnny 38 → 34). Owen: *"Use your backpack thing!"* Tyler reads his sheet, finds **DJ Backpack**, and uses his reaction: *"BASS DROP!"* (they high-five). The phantom is stunned and skips its next strike. *(Johnny USES 1/3.)*
*   **03:20 – 04:30** *Semantics argument:* "the reaper's blade." Tyler says the Coffin: *"Reaper = death = coffin."* Owen says the Scythe: *"A blade is a blade."* On "the first wing," Tyler picks the **Raven** (*"ravens are like THE spooky bird"*); Owen points at the arrow over the Bat and isn't sure. Neither gives in. This takes about 70 seconds.
*   **04:00** Hypothesis 1 (Tyler): *"The dragon's the boss. Boss gets the biggest gear."* With the size-scaled props, the biggest gear is the **8-tooth decoy**. He puts the 8 on Peg 2, the next-biggest 7 on Peg 3, and the two smallest (3 and 4) on Pegs 1 and 4, in reverse order.
*   **04:40 · Wrong crank #1.** Tyler cranks before Owen can stop him. The DM: *"Grrrrind-SHRIEK."* The phantom would strike right away, but it is still stunned from DJ Backpack, so the strike is cancelled. Tyler: *"Backpack carrying the whole team."* Owen groans: *"Dude, you didn't even read the stars."*
*   **05:00 · Phantom tick #2 → Drac.** Attack d20 **7** + 5 = **12** vs AC 14: **miss**. Owen does the voice: *"I do NOT say bleh bleh bleh!"*
*   **05:30** Owen checks his sheet (taking initiative) and announces **Bat Form** (bonus action): *"I fly up to the ceiling to look at the real stars."* The DM delivers **Clue 2 (The Gilded Stars)**. Tyler: *"So not Raven. My bad."* Owen: *"And Scythe, not Coffin. Told you."* Both arguments are settled in about 15 seconds. *(Drac USES 1/3.)* They now know which three constellations matter.
*   **06:15** *Blunder:* Tyler gets bored and tries **Guitar Smack** on the phantom. d20 **1**, a natural 1. Table groan. The DM narrates a snapped string twanging off the armor. Owen: *"Can we please do the puzzle."*
*   **06:30** Owen: *"Okay, Bat, Dragon, Dragon, Scythe. But what do we put on the pegs?"* They start counting stars without knowing why. Tyler counts the Dragon as **6**, because the tail-tip star sits against the rim and he misses it. Owen gets 7. They don't resolve it.

### 07:00 – 11:00 · Deep deduction, ability use, debate
*   **07:00 · Phantom tick #3 → Johnny.** Attack d20 **13** + 5 = **18**: **hit**. Damage 1d6 (**1**) + 2 = **3** (Johnny 34 → 31). Tyler: *"It's chip damage, I'm fine."*
*   **07:10 – 08:40** **This is the stall.** Owen re-reads "let every star bite into brass" twice. *(Sim device for Owen's insight: d20 **5**, needed 11+. The insight doesn't land.)* Owen's theory is that the constellations tell them *which peg spins which way*. Tyler's theory is that gears go in by size order and the stars are just decoration. They place and remove gears several times without cranking, because Owen won't let Tyler crank again. In the strict run, the DM held the clue schedule and did **not** use the "what bites?" nudge here (see recommendations).
*   **08:50** Tyler grabs the crank again, so the DM delivers **Clue 3 (The Crank Inscription)**: *"STAR FOR TOOTH · TWO AND THREE TURN AS ONE · ALL TOGETHER: THE SKY'S HEART."* Owen slams the table: *"STARS ARE TEETH. Bite! Teeth! That's what bite means!"* Tyler: *"Galaxy brain."*
*   **08:55** Owen, wary of the coming 9:00 tick, uses **Look Into My Eyes!** The phantom saves on d20 **8** + 0 = **8** vs DC 15 and **fails**: it is Paralyzed and will skip the 9:00 strike. *(Drac USES 2/3.)* Owen: *"Freeze, tin can."*
*   **09:00 – 10:15** They count: Bat **4**, Scythe **3**. On the Dragon, Tyler says 6 again and Owen says 7. Tyler wants the 6-tooth gear on Peg 2 and a 7 on Peg 3. Owen: *"'Two and three turn as one.' They have to match. And there's only one 6-gear but two 7s."* Then he finds the check: *"The sky's heart... the middle says XXI. That's 21. Four plus seven plus seven plus three... 21!"* Tyler recounts the Dragon slowly with his finger and finds the tail star: *"Oh, there's one on the edge. Seven."*
*   **10:15 – 11:00** They place the gears, arguing about which is "first." Tyler wants to put the Scythe's 3 on Peg 1 *"because it's at the start of the riddle"* (he means the 10 o'clock position near the top). Owen reads line 1 aloud again: *"The first wing... Peg One."* Final placement: **4 · 7 · 7 · 3.**
*   **11:00 · Phantom tick #5 → Johnny.** (The paralysis covered tick #4 at 9:00.) Attack d20 **5** + 5 = **10** vs AC 13: **miss**. Clue 4 trigger check: they're mid-placement with a full, confident set, not stuck. **Clue 4 is held.**

### 11:00 – 15:00 · Solution, door unlocks, celebration
*   **11:20** Owen lets Tyler crank (*"You've earned it. Barely."*). The DM: *"The gears mesh, Dragon turning Dragon, a deep bell tolls, and the phantom is dragged backwards into its clock. The door swings open."* **Stopwatch stopped at 11:20.**
*   **11:25** The DM awards **Crest #3**. Two high-fives, and Tyler does a fake guitar solo. Owen: *"If you hadn't cranked at minute four we'd have been done at ten."* Tyler: *"If I hadn't cranked, my backpack wouldn't have had its hero moment."*
*   Finish state: Johnny 31/38 HP, 1 use spent; Drac 38/38 HP, 2 uses spent. One wrong crank. *Noon Mark* was not offered (they finished after the 8:00 cutoff).

## 4. Physical Prop Usability Report
*   **Visual cues guided thinking: yes.** The chart's arrow over the Bat and the arrow over Peg 1 together were what eventually settled "first wing." The XXI in the center became the self-check Owen used to overrule Tyler's miscount. That's exactly what the chart is for.
*   **Plaque card:** legible. "Wyrm" read as "worm" for a moment, which was a joke rather than a blocker. Keep the gothic font at 14pt or larger.
*   **Star chart:** two problems.
    1.  **Size:** at 8" two players couldn't both see it, and an elbow covered a constellation. **10" is better** (applied).
    2.  **Dragon tail-tip star** touching the rim caused the 6-vs-7 miscount that took about a minute. **Stars need spacing rules** (applied: at least 1/2" apart, at least 3/4" from the rim).
*   **Gear tokens:**
    1.  **Size tracked tooth count** (old spec), which invited Tyler's "boss gets the biggest gear" theory and the 04:40 wrong crank. That's harmless, but it's a false lead the props themselves created. **Mix the sizes** (applied).
    2.  **Overlap:** at 2.5"–4", adjacent gears overlapped on the 11" door board, and two gears kept sliding. **Shrink gears to 2"–2.5" and space pegs about 2.6" apart** (applied).
    3.  **Token count (7):** right. Three decoys matched to three decoy constellations made the decoys feel fair rather than random.
*   **Phantom standee:** worked as a magnet for Tyler's attention, which is what the "pressure, not the main event" coaching is for. One Guitar Smack, then he came back to the puzzle.

## 5. DM Tuning Recommendations

### Pacing adjustments for live play
*   **Use the "What bites?" nudge at 7:00** (it's already in the DM Notes nudge table). The only real stall was the stars-to-teeth link. One non-spoiler question at 7:00 would probably have saved 1–2 minutes and still let them find the aha themselves.
*   **Hand the star chart with the plaque** if your table is slow to explore. In this run, finding the astrolabe cost about 1 minute.
*   **Phantom pressure was light** (7 damage total, 2 of 5 strikes landed, 2 strikes cancelled by abilities). For a fast or rowdy pair that's fine. For a quiet pair, move the ticks to every 90 seconds to add energy, but keep damage at 1d6+2.
*   **The one wrong crank was good drama.** Keep "wrong crank = immediate strike." It made Owen act as the safety brake, which is the right dynamic.

### Emergency time-savers if the group runs slow
1.  **Behind at 5:00** (no chart, no constellation names): deliver Clue 1 and Clue 2 back to back. Use Johnny's History DC 12 route if Drac won't use Bat Form.
2.  **Behind at 8:00:** deliver Clue 3 right away instead of waiting for them to touch the crank.
3.  **Miscount loop** (a 6 and a 7 in the middle): ask "What does the middle of the chart say?" The 21 check fixes it without you giving a number.
4.  **11:00 and stuck:** Clue 4. **13:00:** the frozen phantom points at each correct gear. Crank, award the crest, move on. The other four stations matter more than this one finishing "clean."
5.  **Skip phantom ticks entirely** if the whole session is running more than 5 minutes late. Narrate them as ticking sound only.

## Changes Applied
These problems showed up in the simulation and were fixed in the station file:
1.  **Star chart enlarged from 8" to 10"**, with star-spacing rules (1/4" dots, at least 1/2" apart, at least 3/4" from the rim) and a specific warning about the Dragon's tail-tip star.
2.  **Gear sizes de-linked from tooth count** ("make sure the 8 isn't simply the biggest gear; mix the sizes") to remove the prop-created "biggest gear" false lead.
3.  **Gear tokens shrunk to 2"–2.5" and door-board pegs spaced about 2.6" apart** so four gears fit across the 11" board without overlapping.
4.  **Clarified that a stun or paralysis cancels the phantom's *next* strike, including a crank strike**, since this came up live at 04:40.
5.  **Added DM Tip #3 (watch for the Dragon miscount)** and the **"What bites?" nudge at 7:00** to the coaching tables.
