# Station 4 Playtest: The Mimic Menagerie (Wands and Keys)

*Simulated playtest of the revised station file ([Station_4_Mimic_Menagerie_Wands_and_Keys.md](../Station_4_Mimic_Menagerie_Wands_and_Keys.md)). Dice were rolled with a random number generator before the narrative was written and used in order. Each roll is stated where it happens.*

---

## 1. Simulation Metadata
*   **Station:** 4: The Mimic Menagerie (Wands and Keys; Ashworth, *GM's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons*, Ch 11).
*   **Pair:** Scooby-Doo (Barbarian 5) & Murray Mummy (Druid 5).
*   **Player 1:** **Tyler**, 13, plays **Scooby-Doo**. Enthusiastic, impatient, impulsive, jokey.
*   **Player 2:** **Arjun**, 14, plays **Murray Mummy**. Analytical, reads character sheets, keeps reining Tyler in.
*   **Starting layout:** **B** (I = Real, II = Construct, III = Illusion, IV = Mimic). After the reset: **C** (I = Construct, II = Real, III = Mimic, IV = Illusion).
*   **Total stopwatch solve time:** **10m 50s** (door unlocks), wrap-up at 11:30. **Target 10–15 min: met**, but only because of one reset (about 3 minutes lost). The clean second attempt took about 3m 40s. A careful pair that doesn't blunder would likely land around **7 minutes**, below the target (see Section 5).
*   **Clues used:** 1, 2, 3. Clue 4 was held in reserve and never deployed.
*   **Damage taken:** Scooby 55 → 52, Murray 38 → 35 (construct blast).

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Playtest Transcript

### 00:00–03:00: Entry, first theories, first prop inspection
*   **00:00** DM reads the read-aloud and lays out four identical Alcove cards (I–IV) and four wand cards. The paperclip on the DM sheet is set to Layout B.
*   **00:20** Tyler: "Bro, it's a Zelda key room. Four keys, one's real. I'm grabbing number one." He reaches toward card I.
    Arjun: "Dude. The station is literally called *Mimic* Menagerie. Do NOT touch it."
*   **00:35** DM (first-60-seconds warning): "You reach in... are you sure?" Tyler pulls his hand back with a dramatic "fiiiine."
*   **01:00** Both grab wand cards. Tyler takes Poison and Light; Arjun takes Acid and Mage Hand. Arjun reads the cards aloud: "Range 30 feet. Poison says... one charge?" Tyler isn't listening.
*   **01:30** **Blunder.** Tyler points Poison at Alcove I: "Pew pew, spray and pray." DM (Wand Reaction Matrix, real key): *"The mist beads on the metal and drifts away."* The DM narrates **without collecting the purple charge bead**, so the bead stays on Tyler's card.
    Arjun: "You just used the one-charge wand on a random key."
    Tyler: "It's fine, it said nothing happened, so it's real, right?"
    Arjun: "Nothing happening just means it's not alive."
*   **02:00–02:45** **Distraction:** Tyler decides there must be more wands and asks to search under the stone table and behind the wand rack ("there's always a secret chest"). DM: "Just dust and cobwebs." (The DM doesn't reveal the plaque yet, since that's reserved for 8:00.) About 45 seconds lost.
*   **02:45** **Clue 1 delivered** (no plan yet, and Tyler has eyed Alcove I twice). DM reads the Scorched Floor text.
    Arjun: "So everyone who walked up to the arches got blown up or eaten. We stay behind the studs."
    Tyler: "Like the safe zone in Fall Guys." Arjun touches the gem socket on the Acid card: "Two wands only have one gem. So one shot each."

### 03:00–07:00: First hypothesis, clue, first failure and reaction
*   **03:10** Arjun proposes a plan: "Light finds the fake one, poison finds the alive one, acid finds the machine one. Then Mage Hand the last one." (He works out the mapping from the wand names and Clue 1, without the plaque.)
*   **03:30** Arjun sweeps Bright Light across all four, but doesn't say what he's watching. DM: Perception DC 12. **Arjun rolls d20 = 6, +4 = 10. Fail.** DM: "The light glares off all four keys. Their shadows swing with the beam... mostly. Hard to tell in the glare."
    Arjun groans: "Six. Of course."
*   **03:50** Tyler: "Scooby looks too!" DM asks what he watches. Tyler: "The shadows, duh." (The DM could have auto-succeeded this under the coaching rule but asked for a roll anyway.) **Tyler rolls d20 = 13, +4 = 17. Success.** DM: *"The shadow behind Alcove III stays frozen at the same angle no matter where the beam goes. It's painted on."* DM places the **Illusion Dispelled** tile on Alcove III.
    High-five. Tyler: "Scooby-Doo, detective dog!"
*   **04:30** Remaining: I (poisoned, no reaction), II, IV.
*   **04:45** **Prop confusion:** Tyler tries to poison Alcove II: "I've still got my bead!" DM: "The wand sputters. Empty." Tyler: "But the gem's still here!" They argue for 30 seconds over whether he has a second shot before the DM rules that the charge was spent at 01:30. *(This is the bead-handoff problem; see Changes Applied.)*
*   **05:30** **Clue 2 prompt.** DM: "Scooby, your stomach just growled. Anything on your sheet that a snack helps with?" Arjun scans Scooby's sheet: "Scooby Snack Surge: *sniff out traps and mimics*. Plus ten!"
    The DM tosses Tyler a real mini pretzel. He eats it with an exaggerated "Scooby-dooby-doo!" Big laugh.
    DM: "One question: are you sniffing for something alive, or something fake?" Arjun: "Alive. We already found the fake."
    **Tyler rolls d20 = 4, +14 = 18 vs DC 12. Success.** DM: *"Alcove IV smells like a wet swamp monster that just ate a meatloaf. Something in there is breathing."* DM places the **mimic standee** on Alcove IV. (Revealed by sniff only. It isn't poisoned, so it's still dormant.)
*   **06:00** Remaining: I and II. One is real, one is the construct.
    Arjun: "Use acid. If it melts, it's the bomb."
    Tyler: "Acid will melt the real key too! In Minecraft acid... wait, there's no acid in Minecraft." **Distraction:** they argue for about 70 seconds over whether acid would destroy a silver key, then about whether "silver" means it's actually iron.
*   **07:10** **First failure.** Tyler, out of patience: "It's a fifty-fifty. Coin flip. Mage Hand on II, let's goooo." Arjun: "NO—"
    DM (Construct, lifted): **BOOM.** Everyone within 30 ft makes a DC 12 Dex save.
    **Tyler: d20 = 8, +2 = 10. Fail. Arjun: d20 = 7, +2 = 9. Fail.** Damage **2d4 = 2 + 1 = 3** each (full on a fail).
    Tyler slumps onto the table: "I'm so bad at this." Arjun: "You are literally the worst." (He's laughing.)
    **Reset:** the DM describes the arches grinding, sweeps the Illusion tile and mimic standee back into the box, returns the beads, and moves the paperclip to Layout C. The DM forgets to say that all old information is now scrambled.

### 07:00–11:00: Deep deduction, ability activation, debate
*   **07:30** Tyler: "Okay, but III is still the fake one, right? And IV's the mimic." Arjun: "It shuffled, genius." Tyler: "It said they *rotated*, so maybe they just moved one over?" They argue for about 40 seconds over whether the order was rotated or randomised before the DM confirms: "Everything you learned is scrambled." *(This becomes Changes Applied #1.)*
*   **08:00** Arjun, now careful: "I sweep the light, and I'm watching the shadows." The DM applies the coaching rule (stated intent = automatic success): *"Alcove IV's shadow doesn't move."* Illusion tile goes on IV. This time the DM holds out a hand and takes each bead as it's used.
*   **08:30** **Clue 3 delivered** (they're debating which one-charge wand to spend first). The DM "notices" the plaque under the dust and hands over the brass plaque card.
    Arjun reads it aloud. Tyler: "'One breath, one drop.' Breath is poison 'cause you breathe it, drop is acid 'cause it drips?" Arjun: "Or 'drop' is the key dropping?" **Semantics debate, about 50 seconds.** They land on: breath = poison, drop = acid, "ghost hand" = Mage Hand.
*   **09:00** Snack Surge is used up (one use per short rest). Tyler begs for another pretzel. The DM, following the station rule, offers a normal sniff instead: Perception +4 vs DC 15 for "alive". **Tyler rolls d20 = 11, +4 = 15. Success, exactly.** DM: *"Alcove III smells like wet dog and old meatloaf."* Mimic standee goes on III.
    Tyler: "SCOOBY NOSE IS OP."
*   **09:20** Remaining: I and II. Arjun spells it out, pointing at the cards: "Acid on one. If it melts, that's the bomb and the *other* one is real. If nothing happens, it can't be the mimic, because the mimic's in III, so it's real." Tyler: "Big brain Murray."
*   **09:50** Arjun fires Acid at Alcove I and hands over the green bead. DM (Construct): *"The acid eats through the casing. Gears, a tiny fuse... it fizzles out."* **Disarmed Construct** card goes on I.
*   **10:20** Arjun: "Tyler. Redemption arc. Mage Hand, Alcove II." Tyler, very slowly: "Mage... Hand... Alcove... two."

### 11:00–15:00: Solution, door unlocks, celebration
*   **10:50** DM: *"The spectral hand lifts the key clean off the pedestal. It's heavy, and real. Behind you, the door's lock clunks open."* The DM slides the silver **Crest #4** token across. **Stopwatch stopped: 10:50.**
*   **10:55** Double high-five, then Tyler holds the token up like a Zelda item-get ("da-da-da-DAAA").
*   **11:10** Arjun asks the DM what Alcove II was the first time. The DM says "Different layout. Can't tell you." (The answer key stays secret for the next pair.)
*   **11:30** The pair heads to the next station. **Clue 4 was never deployed:** the reset happened at 07:10, before the "reset after 10:00" bailout threshold, and they were never stuck at 11:00.

## 4. Physical Prop Usability Report
*   **Identical alcove cards: worked.** Tyler asked "which one's shinier?" twice, and the identical art gave the DM nothing to give away. Because the cards never move on a reset, the shuffle was invisible and quick.
*   **Reveal tiles: worked well.** Placing the Illusion tile, mimic standee, and Disarmed Construct card on the alcove cards gave the pair a visible "board state" to reason from. Arjun pointed at the tiles while explaining his final deduction. Sweeping them off on the reset was a clear, dramatic signal (about 15 s).
*   **Charge beads: confusing in the first half.** Tyler kept the purple bead after firing because the DM narrated before collecting it, and that caused a 30-second rules argument at 04:45. Once the DM held out a hand for each bead (second attempt), it worked perfectly. The "ONE CHARGE" banner was noticed by Arjun but not by Tyler, so the bead has to be the physical enforcement. *(Fixed: see Changes Applied #2.)*
*   **Legibility:** the numerals I–IV were readable from across the table. "RANGE 30 FT (6 squares)" on the wand cards wasn't needed (the stud line in the read-aloud covered it), but it did no harm.
*   **Token count:** 4 alcove cards + 4 wand cards + 2 beads + 3 reveal tiles + plaque + Crest token = 15 pieces. That's manageable for two players. The DM box needs separate slots for reveal tiles so the mimic standee doesn't peek out early.
*   **Plaque placement:** keeping it face-down under the wand rack fit the "dusty plaque" reveal naturally. Tyler's "search for secret wands" at 02:00 nearly found it early. That's acceptable; if a player physically searches the rack before 8:00, let them have it (reward the search).
*   **Real snack:** the highest-engagement prop in the room. Bring individually wrapped mini pretzels or crackers and check for allergies beforehand.

## 5. DM Tuning Recommendations

### Pacing adjustments for live play
*   **The puzzle runs short without a mistake.** The clean second attempt took about 3m 40s, and a careful pair would likely solve it in about 7 minutes. Don't add hazards. Instead, use the **early-finish bonus** in the station's DM Tips, or simply bank the time for slower stations (Stations 3 and 5 tend to overrun).
*   **The reset is where the time goes, and that's healthy.** One reset added about 3 minutes and produced the best teaching moment of the run. Don't soften the first reset; do use Clue 4 if a *second* reset happens or a reset comes after 10:00.
*   **Use the "say what you're watching" rule from the first sweep.** The failed Light roll at 03:30 cost about 20 seconds plus Tyler's re-roll. Ask "what are you watching?" before calling for a roll, and if they say "the shadows", it simply works.
*   **Give Clue 2 a little earlier with an impulsive Scooby.** If Player 1 is clearly itching to grab something, the snack prompt at about 04:30 channels that energy into the hook instead of a coin-flip grab.

### Emergency time-savers if the group runs slow
1.  **At 11:00 or after a second reset:** deploy Clue 4 (Murray's Bandage Tap) in full.
2.  **Skip the mimic fight:** if the mimic is triggered after 10:00, Murray's Shuffle restrains it and it scuttles into the wall. No damage rolls.
3.  **Skip the reshuffle:** after 12:00, a wrong alcove triggers its hazard but **doesn't shuffle**. Leave all reveal tiles in place.
4.  **Glowing key at 13:00:** the real key glows faintly, and they solve by 14:00.
5.  **Hard stop at 15:00:** hand over Crest #4 with a funny cosmetic cost and move on.

## Changes Applied
The simulation exposed two running problems. Both are fixed in the station file's **DM Tips & Running Recommendations**:
1.  **Reset read-aloud that says "everything is scrambled" (Tip 2).** At 07:30 the players argued for about 40 s over whether old reveals still counted, or whether the alcoves had only rotated one step. The new reset read-aloud says outright that all prior information is scrambled and that the wands are recharged.
2.  **Enforce the charge-bead handoff (Tip 3).** At 04:45 Tyler still had his bead after firing Poison, because the DM narrated before collecting it, which led to a 30 s rules argument. The DM now holds out a hand for the bead *before* narrating any Poison or Acid result.

*No changes were needed to DCs, damage, clue wording, or layouts; they performed as intended in this run.*
