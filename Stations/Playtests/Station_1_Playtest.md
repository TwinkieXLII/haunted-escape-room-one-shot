# Station 1 Playtest: The Library — The Crown and the Dagger

Simulated playtest of [Station 1](../Station_1_Library_Crown_and_Dagger.md). Dice were rolled with a random number generator before the run and used in order; every roll is stated below.

---

## 1. Simulation Metadata
*   **Station:** 1, The Library (The Crown and the Dagger, *Game Master's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons*, Ch 11).
*   **Pair:** Fred Jones (Fighter 5; Investigation +4, Survival +3, Wis +0, 44 HP) & Griffin the Invisible Man (Rogue 5; Investigation +5, Sleight of Hand +7, Wis +0, 36 HP).
*   **Players:**
    *   **Player 1, "Jayden" (14), plays Fred:** enthusiastic, impatient, impulsive, jokey.
    *   **Player 2, "Marcus" (15), plays Griffin:** analytical, reads his sheet, keeps telling Jayden to wait.
*   **Stopwatch solve time:** **10m 40s** (gate opens). Station complete including celebration: 12m 00s.
*   **Target:** 10–15 minutes. **Result: within target, at the low end.** Clue 4 was never needed. Good advantage rolls on the hook kept it quick; see the variance notes in Section 5.
*   **Dice log (d20 in order):** 19, 9, 10, 17/1, 4/14, 7, 16, 3, 5, 3. **d6:** 5.

## 2. Minute-by-Minute Playtest Transcript

### 00:00–03:00 — Entry, first theories, first prop inspection
*   **00:00** DM reads the read-aloud. The six statue cards stand in a row (Barov's propped on a paperback), and the six relic tokens sit in a pile on a felt "runner."

*   **00:50** Jayden grabs the Dagger token and slaps it on Victoria's card. "She looks evil. Done." Marcus: "Dude, we haven't looked at anything." Not a taboo pair, so the DM skips the warning line.

*   **02:40–03:00** First theory (Marcus): "It's six jobs. Scholar, tiller, smith, sailor, killer, and... 'one.' Each statue is one of these."

*   **04:30** Marcus (Griffin) inspects Victoria. **Roll 10 + 5 = 15.** Slip 1: ink-black fingertips. "Scholar reads. Book." **Book on Victoria: correct.**
*   **05:15** **Clue 2 nudge.** DM: *"Fred, your sheet says you love splitting up. Anything on there that fits?"* Jayden: "LET'S SPLIT UP, GANG!" in a Fred voice. Marcus objects that it's a move ability; the DM gives the station ruling (both inspect different statues with Advantage).
*   **05:30** Split-up round:
    *   Fred on Alexi: **Advantage 17 / 1, take 17 + 4 = 21.** Slip 2: dried mud on boots and hem. Jayden: "Imagine if I rolled the 1."
    *   Griffin on Valerius: **Advantage 4 / 14, take 14 + 5 = 19.** Slip 4: salt smell, barnacles.
*   **06:15** Marcus: "Mud is the tiller. Scythe." **Scythe on Alexi: correct.** Three placed.
*   **06:30** Debate over Valerius. Marcus: "Sailor... spear? Like a harpoon?" Jayden: "No, ship guys build ships. Hammer. Nails." He places the Hammer on Valerius before Marcus finishes.

Above the fireplace, the gargoyle's eyes ignite..."* Shriek: Fred DC 12 Wis save, **roll 7 + 0 = 7, fail. 1d6 psychic = 5** (Fred 44 → 39 HP). Griffin isn't targeted. Marcus taps his sheet: "Now You Don't. Permanently invisible. It can't see me." DM confirms. Marcus: "Best. Feature." Jayden: "You're the worst."

*   **07:00–07:30** Groaning and re-placing. Marcus rebuilds the board from the slips: dagger on Teresa, book on Victoria, scythe on Alexi. All three stick. Having the slips under the cards made the rebuild fast. Without them, the players would have had to remember.
*   **07:50** Fred inspects Borislav. **Roll 16 + 4 = 20.** Slip 5: soot. Both: "Smith burns. HAMMER." **Hammer on Borislav: correct.** Four placed.

I float the hand up to Barov's plinth and feel his hands." **Problem found:** the draft called for a DC 13 roll. **Roll 3 + 5 = 8, fail.** Barov has no stain to find, so a failure would have dead-ended the last statue. The DM improvised: *"Your hand comes back... clean. Not even dust. Every other statue left grit on your fingers."* Slip 6 handed over. Marcus: "'One sits clean above the pain.' That's him." ## 4. Physical Prop Usability Report
*   **Visual cues guided thinking: yes.** The Telling Detail slips tucked under each statue card became the players' working "evidence board." After the all-drop at 06:40, Marcus rebuilt three correct placements in 30 seconds by reading the slips. This is the most important prop in the room.
*   **Barov's raised card (propped on a paperback):** made "out of reach" physical, which set up the Mage Hand moment on its own. Keep it.
*   **Neutral card art and first-name labels:** worked. Neither player could match by costume or title, so the inspection loop carried the puzzle. The flipped-card reveal of full titles at the end got the biggest laugh of the session.
*   **Relic tokens (6 x 2.5"):** easy to grab and place; the count was never confusing. The sea kelp on the Spear token wasn't noticed until after the solve. It's harmless and subtle, so leave it.
*   **Legibility:** the inscription handout needs 16 pt+ type. Fancy gothic calligraphy slowed the read-aloud; Marcus squinted at "tiller." The slips (14 pt) read fine.
*   **Layout:** six 4" x 6" cards in a row need about 30" of table. Put the relic pile in front of the cards, not off to the side, so the "all drop" sweep is visible.
*   **Gargoyle:** no prop needed. Narration was enough. A gargoyle mini on the "fireplace" (a box lid) would make the Net Trap derail more fun, but it's optional.

## 5. DM Tuning Recommendations

### Pacing adjustments for live play
*   **Expect variance of about ±3 minutes from rolls.** This run had a lucky 17 and 14 on the Advantage round. With average luck, add 1–2 failed inspections (about 1 minute). A cautious pair who inspects all six before placing anything could finish around 8–9 minutes, which is *under* target. If a pair is clearly racing, hold the Clue 1 nudge until 03:00 and let the "never earned" debate breathe.
*   **Don't stop the first impulsive grab.** The 01:05 clang taught the all-drop rule better than any explanation.
*   **Cap derails at 1 minute.** Both derails (gargoyle net, "tiller") were fun and self-limiting at about a minute each; that's the right budget.
*   **Use the taboo warning line on every crown or dagger reach,** right or wrong, so it never confirms anything.

### Emergency time-savers if the group runs slow
1.  **Auto-succeed inspections** after one failed roll on the same statue ("you try a new angle and spot it").
2.  **Reveal Clue 3 at 07:00** instead of 09:00.
3.  **Suspend the all-drop rule** (only the wrong item drops) if they've had two misplacements before 08:00.
4.  **Clue 4 partial at 11:00** (only the unlocked pairs), full at 13:00.
5.  **Skip the Spectre fight** if a Taboo Swap happens after 12:00: it appears, wails, and fades, leaving Clue 4 behind.

## Changes Applied
Fixes made to the station file because of this simulation:
1.  **Barov needs no inspection roll.** At 09:45 a failed DC 13 roll on the spotless statue would have left nothing to find and dead-ended the final pair. Now any close look (Mage Hand, climb, or a stare after Clue 3) automatically finds "nothing, not even dust," and the absence is the clue.
2.  **Clue 3 auto-reveals at 09:00.** Both plinth rolls failed (8 and 9 vs DC 12). The 09:00 auto-reveal, improvised in the run, is now written into the clue trigger and the pacing table.
3.  **Clue 4 trigger narrowed to the Taboo Swap or 11:00.** The second misplacement happened at 06:40; under the earlier "2 failed attempts" trigger, Clue 4 would have handed over the full answer with four minutes of good deduction still ahead. The second misplacement now triggers only the gargoyle shriek.
4.  **Failed inspections allow a retry with a new approach** so no clue is locked behind one bad roll (book anti-pattern).
