# Station 1: The Library — The Crown and the Dagger

## Source & Metadata
*   **Source:** *The Game Master's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons* (Jeff Ashworth), Ch 11 Gateway Puzzles, "The Crown and the Dagger" (chapter spans pp. 122–141; exact page not recorded). Replaces the 8-card Chronology puzzle.
*   **Book mechanics kept:** six stone items matched to six statues by the telling detail on each (ink-stained: book; muddy shoes: scythe; bloody hands: dagger; seawater: spear; soot: hammer; spotless: crown). A wrong item makes all placed items drop. A dagger/crown mix-up releases the book's wraith, run here as a weaker **Spectre**. The book's DC 15 Perception/Investigation is lowered to DC 13 for this table.
*   **Alternate:** *Planning for War* (Ch 12).
*   **Estimated Playtime:** 10–15 minutes (Simulated Playtest: **10m 40s** solve; low end of the target. See [Station 1 Playtest](Playtests/Station_1_Playtest.md)).
*   **Type:** Visual observation, environmental deduction, and physical item-matching.
*   **Players:** Fred Jones (Fighter 5) & Griffin the Invisible Man (Rogue 5).
*   **Goal:** Place the 6 relic items into the correct ancestor statues to unlock the Archive Gate and release **Crest #1**.

> **Visibility key:** sections marked **[DM ONLY]** stay behind the screen. Only the items listed under **Player-Facing Props & Handouts** go on the table.

## DM Notes (Coaching) [DM ONLY]

### What this room is testing
This room tests whether the players *look before they act*. Every statue carries a sign of the life it lived (ink, mud, blood, brine, soot, or nothing at all), and every relic is the tool that left that sign. The players win by inspecting statues, connecting each sign to a tool, and resisting the urge to brute-force. The twist is the last pair: the crown goes to the statue with *no* sign at all, and swapping the crown and the dagger is the one mistake that bites. Your job is to make the details easy to notice and the cost of guessing easy to feel, while never saying which item goes where.

### How to describe the room
*   **Read-aloud (00:00):** *"Dust hangs in the candlelight. Six stone ancestors stand in alcoves, grey with a century of grime, their empty hands held out as if waiting for a gift. On the reading table under a rumpled velvet runner lie six stone relics. Above the fireplace, a stone gargoyle's eyes seem to follow whoever touches the table. One statue, its plaque reading only 'Barov', stands higher than the rest on a tall plinth."*
*   **Emphasize:** the word *dust* (every statue but one is dusty), the *empty hands*, the *rumpled runner* (Clue 1 is under it), the gargoyle watching the *table*, and Barov's *tall plinth* (out of reach for a close look).
*   **Underplay:** statue names and costumes. The player-facing cards show first names only; do not read out the full titles (the "Traitor", "Harvester", etc.) until the gate opens.

### Nudge lines by stage (questions only, never answers)
| Stage | When | Say aloud |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Orientation | 02:00–03:00, if no one has touched the table | *"That velvet runner is lumpy. Anyone curious?"* / *"What do you do with a table full of relics in a library?"* |
| Inspection | 03:00–05:00, if they are guessing instead of looking | *"These are statues of real people. What would a statue remember about the person?"* / *"Who wants to get up close to one?"* |
| Character hook | 05:00–06:00 | Fred: *"Your sheet says you love splitting up. Anything on there that fits?"* Griffin: *"Barov's hands are way up there. Anything on your sheet that reaches far?"* |
| Logic | 08:00–09:00 | *"You've found five stains. What's strange about the sixth statue?"* / *"Have you checked the plinths they stand on?"* |
| Taboo warning | Before any crown or dagger placement | *"The gargoyle's eyes flare red. Are you sure?"* (Say it every time, right or wrong, so it gives nothing away.) |

### What NOT to say or do
*   Never name an item-statue pair, and never confirm a pairing before the token is placed. "Try it and see" is fine.
*   Never point at Barov and say "this one is spotless" before Clue 3. After Clue 3, you may say *"Look closely at what isn't there."*
*   Don't read the full statue titles or the Solution Matrix aloud. Don't let the matrix or the Clue 4 text sit where the players can see it.
*   Don't call for a reroll as a punishment. A failed inspection costs time, not the clue (see Inspection Rules).
*   Don't hand over Clue 4 early because the players are noisy. It waits for a Taboo Swap or 11:00.

### Pacing checkpoints
| Minute | Expected state | If behind, do this |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 03:00 | Clue 1 in hand | Give the orientation nudge, then reveal the inscription as the runner slides off the table on its own. |
| 06:00 | 2–4 statues inspected, Clue 2 hook used | Prompt the hook directly by reading its name off their sheet. |
| 09:00 | 4–5 relics placed | Reveal Clue 3 automatically (the plinth plate cracks free). |
| 11:00 | Still stuck | Deploy Clue 4, giving only the pairs they haven't locked in. |
| 13:00 | Not solved | Clue 4 in full, then let them place the tokens. Gate opens by 15:00. |

### Handling common derails
*   **Impulsive player grabs a relic at 00:30:** Let them handle and inspect it freely. Picking up, touching, moving or inspecting a relic triggers nothing. Only placing it with the wrong statue causes the clang and reset. Before any crown or dagger placement, give the same neutral warning; release the Spectre only for dagger to Barov or crown to Teresa.
*   **Combat urges (Net Trap on the gargoyle, attacking statues):** Allow one round of fun. The gargoyle is stone until triggered; the net drapes over it and it blinks. Then pull them back with an inspection nudge. Cap derails at 1 minute.
*   **Brute-forcing by trying every relic:** The all-drop rule plus the gargoyle shriek makes this costly. Narrate the shriek getting louder each time.
*   **Clever alternative solutions (reward them, as the book says):** smelling the statues, rubbing dust off with a sleeve, checking which relic is "heaviest with use", using Fred's Survival (+3) to read the mud as farmland mud. Give the detail without a roll, or with Advantage. If they argue a pairing logically and correctly, let it stick.
*   **Semantic arguments over the rhyme ("tiller" = cash register?):** Let it run 1 minute, then ask *"What does a tiller do in a field?"*

### Adjudicating the character hooks
*   **Fred, "Let's Split Up!"** (sheet: move with a friend safely). Station ruling: once per station, Fred and his partner split to two different statues and *both* roll their inspection with Advantage that round.
*   **Griffin, "Now You Don't"** (passive invisibility). The gargoyle only shrieks at creatures it can *see*, so Griffin never takes the shriek damage. Point this out the first time it fires; it's a great moment for the Griffin player.
*   **Griffin, "Mage Hand Shenanigans"** (move small items from 6 squares). Inspects Barov's tall plinth with no climb, and can float the crown into Barov's raised hands. Without it, a climb is Athletics DC 12.
*   **Fred's Survival / Griffin's Sleight of Hand:** accept them as alternate inspection skills on fitting statues (mud tracks; picking dried blood from under fingernails).

### Keep it off the table
The **Solution Matrix**, the **Clue 4 text**, and the statue **full titles** are DM eyes only. Print the matrix on the back of your DM screen, not on a handout.

## Room Setup
*   Six stone statues of Von Zarovich ancestors stand in alcoves along the library wall, each with outstretched empty hands and a coat of dust. Statue 6 (Barov) stands on a tall plinth, hands raised above the others.
*   Six stone relics sit on the central reading table under a velvet runner: **Book, Scythe, Dagger, Spear, Hammer, Crown**.
*   A stone gargoyle above the fireplace watches the reading table. It wakes after the second misplacement.
*   Each statue shows the subtle physical evidence of how that ancestor lived or met their end, revealed by inspection (see Inspection Rules).

### Inspection Rules [DM ONLY]
*   **Inspecting a statue:** Investigation DC 13 (Fred +4, Griffin +5). Success: place that statue's **Telling Detail slip** under its card.
*   **Failed inspection:** "You see only dust and stone. Try a different approach." The player may retry next turn with a new approach (touch, smell, a candle, a sleeve). The clue is never locked behind one failed roll.
*   **Barov (Statue 6): no roll.** Any close inspection (Mage Hand, a climb, or a long stare from below after Clue 3) finds *nothing*: no stain, not even dust. The absence is the clue. Hand over his slip.

## Failure Consequences [DM ONLY]
*   **Placement trigger only:** Handling and inspecting relics is safe. A placement means offering a relic to a statue (putting the token in or beside its hands/card, or declaring the offering). Merely moving a token around the table is not a placement.
*   **Any Misplacement (book rule):** The statue shudders and *every* relic placed so far clatters back onto the table with an echoing clang. The players must re-place the ones they had right.
*   **Second and Later Misplacements:** The gargoyle wakes and shrieks at everyone it can see: DC 12 Wis save or take 1d6 psychic damage. Griffin is invisible and is never targeted.
*   **The Taboo Swap (Crown & Dagger):** Placing the Dagger in Barov's hands or the Crown in Teresa's releases a minor **Spectre** (AC 12, 14 HP, +4 to hit, 1d6 necrotic), scaled so Level 5 teens can dispatch it in 1 round. This is the "critical mistake" that unlocks Clue 4 early.

## The 4 Progressive Clues & Handouts

### Clue 1: The Reading Table Inscription (Sensory / Environmental)
*   **Timing:** Minute 2–3, if they need orientation.
*   **Trigger:** Inspecting the central table (Investigation DC 11) or lifting the velvet runner (no roll).
*   **Text/Handout (Prop Handout 1):**
    > *"Six walked before the master. The scholar reads while the tiller reaps; the smith burns while the sailor weeps. The killer's hands are scarlet stained, while one sits clean above the pain."*
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** Tells the players the six statues are six *roles* and hints at their stains, without saying which statue is which or naming the crown's owner ("one", not "the king").

### Clue 2: The Close Inspection (Character Hook)
*   **Timing:** Minute 5–6, when they take initiative to inspect.
*   **Trigger:** Fred uses *"Let's Split Up!"* (both partners inspect different statues with Advantage that round) or Griffin uses *Mage Hand Shenanigans* to reach Barov's tall plinth.
*   **Text/Handout:** The **Telling Detail slips** for the statues inspected (see Player-Facing Props, item 2).
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** Speeds up gathering the evidence and rewards reading the character sheet. The players still have to connect each detail to a tool.

### Clue 3: The Plinth Engravings (Logic Constraint)
*   **Timing:** Minute 8–9.
*   **Trigger:** Inspecting any plinth (Investigation DC 12). **If no one finds it by 09:00, reveal it automatically:** a plinth plate cracks loose and slides out.
*   **Text/Handout (Prop Handout 3):**
    > *"I. The hand is marked by what it held.*
    > *II. The hand that never worked shall hold what was never earned."*
*   **Calibration:** **Just Helpful Enough.** Line I gives the rule (stain = tool). Line II points the crown, the one relic nobody earns by work, to the statue with no stain, without naming either one. This guards against the Taboo Swap.

### Clue 4: Ghost Librarian's Whisper (Emergency Fail-Forward) [DM ONLY]
*   **Trigger:** Deployed **only** after a Taboo Swap (critical mistake) or at **11:00** if the players are stuck.
*   **Text (spoken, never handed out):**
    > *A translucent librarian floats past, muttering: 'Give the book to the ink, the scythe to the mud, the hammer to the soot, the spear to the brine, the crown to the spotless one, and the dagger to the blood!'*
*   **Delivery:** At 11:00, speak only the pairs they haven't locked in yet. Give the full line only at 13:00.
*   **Calibration:** **Too Helpful (Fail-Forward by design).** Bypasses the remaining deduction so the station finishes inside the 15-minute budget.

## Player-Facing Props & Handouts (Ready for Generation)
*Use these prompt descriptions for an image-generation agent or graphic designer to make the physical table assets. Everything in this section may go on the table except where marked.*

### 1. The Six Ancestor Statue Cards / Standees
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 4" x 6" vertical tarot-sized cards or folding paper standees. Prop Barov's card on a small box or book stack to show his tall plinth.
*   **Art Style:** Dark gothic oil painting style, grey dusty stone, cold tones, atmospheric candlelit background. All six figures wear similar noble robes so costume alone doesn't give the answer. The telling details are **not** shown on the cards; they come on the slips.
*   **Front label:** number and first name only. **Back (DM reveals on solve):** full title.
*   **Card 1 (Victoria):** Female figure in high-collared Barovian robes, hands outstretched palms up. Back: *"Arch-Librarian Victoria"*.
*   **Card 2 (Alexi):** Rugged nobleman in riding boots and a long coat. Back: *"Lord Alexi the Harvester"*.
*   **Card 3 (Teresa):** Aristocratic woman with a cold gaze, wearing lace cuffs. Back: *"Lady Teresa the Traitor"*.
*   **Card 4 (Valerius):** Weathered man in tarnished plate armor under a heavy cloak. Back: *"Admiral Valerius"*.
*   **Card 5 (Borislav):** Broad-shouldered man in a long work coat. Back: *"Forge-Master Borislav"*.
*   **Card 6 (Barov):** Proud, regal figure in long robes on a tall plinth, hands raised. Back: *"King Barov the Spotless"*.

### 2. The Six Telling Detail Slips
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 2" x 3.5" (business-card size), large print (14 pt+), each numbered to match its statue. Hand one over per successful inspection and tuck it under that statue card.
    *   **1 Victoria:** "Black ink is ground into her fingertips and thumbs."
    *   **2 Alexi:** "Dried, flaking mud is caked on his boots and coat hem."
    *   **3 Teresa:** "Dark crimson stains her palms and the skin under her fingernails."
    *   **4 Valerius:** "He smells of salt and brine. Tiny barnacles cling to his shoulder plates."
    *   **5 Borislav:** "Black soot smears his jaw, knuckles and forearms."
    *   **6 Barov:** "Nothing. No stain, no grime, not even dust. He gleams as if polished this morning."

### 3. The Six Stone Relic Cutout Tokens
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 2.5" x 2.5" square cutout tokens or poker-chip sized markers.
*   **Art Style:** Carved basalt stone relics with subtle magical runes.
*   **Token A (Book):** Leather-bound grimoire with a brass clasp and weathered parchment pages.
*   **Token B (Scythe):** Curved agricultural scythe with an ashwood handle and iron blade.
*   **Token C (Dagger):** Cruel, wavy-bladed stiletto dagger with an ornate ruby pommel.
*   **Token D (Spear):** Iron boar spear wrapped in wet leather cord and sea kelp.
*   **Token E (Hammer):** Heavy square-headed blacksmith forging hammer with an iron-banded grip.
*   **Token F (Crown):** Gilded spiked royal circlet set with black onyx gemstones.

### 4. Handout Props
*   **Prop Handout 1 (Clue 1, The Reading Table Inscription):** Parchment handout (8.5" x 5.5") with torn, burned edges and elegant gothic calligraphy (keep it legible, 16 pt+):
    > *"Six walked before the master. The scholar reads while the tiller reaps; the smith burns while the sailor weeps. The killer's hands are scarlet stained, while one sits clean above the pain."*
*   **Prop Handout 2 (Archive Crest #1):** A 3-inch circular metallic silver token depicting a raven perched on an open book, with interlocking puzzle teeth on its outer rim. Given when the gate opens.
*   **Prop Handout 3 (Clue 3, Plinth Plate):** A small grey "stone plate" card (3" x 5") engraved with the two lines of Clue 3.
*   **Prop 4 (Spectre Mini / Standee) [DM ONLY until released]:** A 2-inch spectral combat standee of a howling translucent blue Spectre for the Taboo Swap.

## DM Tips & Running Recommendations
1.  **Hands-On Tokens:** Let the players physically grab the relic tokens and place them on the statue cards. When an item is wrong, sweep *all* placed tokens back to the table yourself, loudly. Physical manipulation keeps teens engaged.
2.  **The Spotless Statue Comes Last:** Don't point at Barov early. After Clue 3 (08:00+), if they still stall, have torchlight flicker across Statue 6: *"Every statue has grit or grime on it... what about this one?"*
3.  **Spectre Combat Buffer:** If they release the Spectre, Fred's *Net Trap* (DC 13 Dex) Restrains it and Griffin's Invisible Strike with Sneak Attack (1d8+4 +3d6, always with Advantage) finishes it in about 60 seconds. Then deliver Clue 4.
