# Station 5: The Collapsing Armory — Corridor of the Taboo Word

> **DM EYES ONLY.** This whole file is for the DM. The only things players may see are the items marked **[PLAYER HANDOUT]** in the Visual Props section (and the Clue handouts, only when they are triggered). Keep this sheet behind the DM screen.

## Source & Metadata
*   **Source:** *Corridor of the Taboo Word*, *The Game Master's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons* (Jeff Ashworth), Ch 11 Gateway Puzzles, pp. 122–141 (exact page not recorded). Book mechanics: cross a 200-ft corridor without saying "trap"; investigate flashing foreign words (Investigation 12, language rolled on 1d8); each slip rolls 1d8 on a hazard table; the door locks for 5 minutes per slip. Here merged with the collapsing spiked ceiling as a timer, and the flashing words now spell the door's password.
*   **Alternate:** *The Barbarian's Brilliance* (Ch 11).
*   **Estimated Playtime:** 10–15 minutes (Simulated playtest: **12m 10s**, inside target. See [Station 5 Playtest Report](Playtests/Station_5_Playtest.md)).
*   **Type:** Spoken-word taboo challenge + glyph-matching deduction + cooperative brace-and-fetch under a timer.
*   **Players:** Frankenstein / Frank (Barbarian 5) & Wayne Werewolf (Barbarian 5).
*   **Goal:** Cross the armory without speaking the forbidden word, stop the spiked ceiling, work out the door's password from the flashing words, speak it to open the blast door, and retrieve **Crest #5** from the drainage grate.

## DM Notes (Coaching)
*DM-only. Nothing in this section is read aloud.*

### What this room is testing
This room tests three things at once: **self-control under pressure** (can two excited teens keep from saying one word while things are going wrong?), **observation and pattern matching** (five glowing words on the ceiling match five of eight weapon-rack tags; the first letters of those weapons, read from the entrance to the door, spell the password **SNARE**), and **using their character sheets** (Frank's *Massive Shoulders* stops the ceiling; Wayne's *Wolf Senses* and *Swarm of Pups* get the crest out of a grate no hand can fit through). The taboo word is the comedy; the matching is the puzzle; the ceiling is the clock.

### Introducing the taboo rule
*   Read the archway plaque aloud and hand it over. Then say the rule plainly, once, out of character: *"There's a word you can't say in here. It's the word for the thing this whole castle is full of. It's on the plaque as T-dash-dash-dash. If anyone at the table says it out loud, in character or not, I ring the bell and roll on the bad-stuff table."*
*   Confirm they understand which word it is **without saying it**: point at the "T---" on the plaque and let them nod. Don't make them say it to confirm (that would count).
*   **Baiting without being mean:** set up situations, don't trick them with gotchas. Good bait: describe a pressure plate or a tripwire and ask *"What do you think that is?"*; describe a loose flagstone and ask *"What do you call that?"*. Bad bait: asking direct yes/no questions designed only to force the word, or accusing them of slips they didn't make. If a player is getting frustrated (two slips in a row), stop baiting for a few minutes.
*   **Free laugh:** Frank's sheet describes *Massive Shoulders* with the taboo word in it. If Frank's player reads it aloud word for word, that counts. Don't warn them; just ring the bell with a big grin. It's the funniest moment in the station.

### Running the ceiling timer visibly
*   Start a phone stopwatch the moment you finish the read-aloud. Every **real minute** on the minute, slide the ceiling token down **1 ft** on the tracker and grind your teeth or scrape a chair for the sound.
*   Say the height aloud every time: *"Thirteen feet. Grrrrind."* The kids should never have to ask.
*   The ceiling stops the moment Frank uses *Massive Shoulders*. Say so dramatically and leave the token where it is.
*   Use the 1-minute sand timer for the door lockout after each slip, and put it on the table next to the door so they can see it.

### Nudge lines (non-spoiler, say aloud)
Questions that redirect attention, never the answer:
*   **Stuck at the start (0–3 min):** *"The ceiling's coming down and the words on it are glowing. What does the light do when it pulses?"* / *"How many racks are there? How many words?"*
*   **Matching the cards (3–7 min):** *"Do any of those squiggles look like anything else in the room?"* / *"Who in the party has the loudest muscles for that ceiling?"* (point at Frank, not at the ability).
*   **Have the matches, no password (7–10 min):** *"You've got five weapons. What's the door waiting for?"* / *"Read your plaque again. What does it say you're allowed to talk about?"*
*   **At the grate:** *"Your hands won't fit. Who in this party is small enough?"* (to Wayne) / *"You've got the best nose and ears in the castle. Which way do they point you?"*

### What NOT to say or do
*   **The DM never says the taboo word.** Not in read-aloud, not in rules talk, not in jokes. Call it "the word", "T-word" or point at the plaque. If you slip, ring the bell on yourself and roll the d8 on the nearest player for the laugh, but use the mild result (re-roll a 1, 5 or 7).
*   Never say "SNARE", "first letters" or "in order" until Clue 3 is due. Never point at a specific rack.
*   Never tell Frank to use *Massive Shoulders* or Wayne to use *Swarm of Pups* by name. Point them at their sheets instead.
*   Don't let damage drag the room: roll in the open, read the number, move on. Nobody goes to 0 HP in this room.

### Pacing checkpoints
| Clock | Where they should be | If they're behind |
|---|---|---|
| **00:00–01:00** | Rule explained, plaque handed over, timer running. | Read faster. |
| **02:30** | Looking at the ceiling words or the racks. | Give **Clue 1**. |
| **05:00–06:00** | Frank has inspected the ceiling or the pair is matching cards. | Give **Clue 2** when they take any initiative toward the ceiling. |
| **08:00–09:00** | At least 3 cards matched to racks. | Give **Clue 3** (door sockets light up). |
| **11:00** | Password spoken, or working the grate. | If stuck, give **Clue 4** (the matching variant). |
| **13:00** | Frank diving for the door. | Hand-wave the last checks; narrate the escape. |
| **15:00** | Hard stop. | Ceiling reaches 0 ft: fail-forward ending (see Room Setup). |

### Handling derails
*   **Saying the word on purpose for laughs:** it still counts, every time. Ring the bell and roll. If one player is doing it to annoy the other, the third deliberate slip in a minute is always result **8 (Ceiling Surge)**, and say so: the room "gets bored of the joke".
*   **Arguing about variants:** use the ruling table below and don't relitigate. One sentence, then move on: *"Strap is fine. Trapdoor counts. Spelling it counts."*
*   **Clever alternatives (the book says reward them):** saying the word in another real language ("piège", "Falle", "trampa"), miming it, writing it on paper, or the analytical player muzzling the impulsive one with a hand are all **allowed and should get praise**. Ripping out the grate, prying the door, or climbing out of the ceiling's path get a fair roll but the room resists (grate and door are fused to the bedrock). If someone guesses SNARE early from the plaque alone, let it work and enjoy it.
*   **Off-task (wrong rack, wrong letters):** give it a minute. Arguing over letters *is* the puzzle. Step in only when the same wrong idea has been repeated three times.

### Adjudicating the character hooks
*   **Frank — *Massive Shoulders* (Action, 1 use):** automatic success, no roll. Frank holds the ceiling perfectly as long as he stays braced under the gargoyle boss. While braced he can't move or take other actions, but **Frank's player can still talk, handle cards and sort tags**. Frank's sheet also gives him advantage on all Strength checks to lift or break.
*   **Wayne — *Wolf Senses* (Passive):** advantage on any check that uses hearing or smell. At the grate, a Perception check (DC 10, with advantage) tells him which pipe the crest is in.
*   **Wayne — *Swarm of Pups* (Action, 1 use):** the pups fit through the 4-inch bars and fetch small items. **One use keeps the pups around for the whole station**, so a wrong pipe costs time (about a minute), not the ability. (The overview notes list this hook under "Exhausted Dad Rage"; on the character sheet it is its own power, *Swarm of Pups*. *Exhausted Dad Rage* is just Wayne's half-damage rage.)

### Keep the answer key off the table
The racks-to-letters key and the word **SNARE** live only on this sheet. Print the rack tags and ceiling cards without any English translation on the back. Don't leave this file open on a laptop the players can see.

## Room Setup
*   A **60-foot** armory corridor (the book's is 200 ft; shortened to fit the station) lined with **eight weapon racks**, four on each side, leads to a sealed adamantine blast door.
*   **Rack tags:** each rack has a brass tag naming its weapon in a different script (the book rolls the language on 1d8; here they are pre-assigned so the props can be printed). In order from the entrance (1) to the door (8):

| # | Rack (English) | Tag script | Lit by a ceiling word? |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---:|
| 1 | **S**pear | Dwarvish | **Yes** |
| 2 | Mace | Giant | No |
| 3 | **N**et | Goblin | **Yes** |
| 4 | **A**xe | Draconic | **Yes** |
| — | *(Central floor grate, under the gargoyle boss)* | | |
| 5 | Glaive | Gnomish | No |
| 6 | **R**apier | Elvish | **Yes** |
| 7 | Halberd | Sylvan *(decoy: looks a lot like Elvish)* | No |
| 8 | **E**xecutioner's Blade | Infernal | **Yes** |

*   **The flashing words:** five glowing words are carved into the underside of the descending ceiling, scattered out of order. Each word is the same weapon name written in the same script as one rack tag. Matching a word to its tag (by eye, or Investigation DC 12 on a card to read it outright) shows which racks are "lit".
*   **The answer (DM only):** take the first letter of each lit weapon in corridor order, entrance to door: **S-N-A-R-E**. Speaking **"SNARE"** aloud at the blast door opens it. Wrong passwords do nothing but make the door's stone mouth cough dust.
*   **The grate:** at the center of the floor sits an iron drainage grate (bars spaced **4 inches** apart, fused into the bedrock). **Crest #5** fell through it on its brass chain into the culvert below. Three pipes branch off underneath; the crest is in the **right** one.
*   **The Hazard (Timer):** a **4-ton** ceiling bristling with 2-foot iron spikes. The spike tips start **15 ft** above the floor and drop **1 ft every real minute** (one tracker notch per minute). At **5 ft** everyone must crouch (half speed). At **0 ft** (minute 15) it crushes: fail-forward, the floor tilts and dumps everyone out through the door in a heap (2d6 piercing each, nobody drops below 1 HP). If the crest wasn't retrieved, the pups (if summoned) or a rush of drain water spits it out after them, dented.
*   **Stopping it:** Frank's *Massive Shoulders* under the central gargoyle boss stops the ceiling where it is.

## The Taboo Word Rule
**The forbidden word is "TRAP".** The archway geas hears anything **spoken aloud at the table** by a player, in character or out of character.

| Counts (ring the bell) | Does NOT count |
|:---|:---|
| trap, traps, trapped, trapping, trapper | strap, trapeze, Trapper Keeper* (*DM call; be generous) |
| trapdoor, booby-trap, mousetrap, "it's a trap!" | the word in another real language (piège, Falle, trampa) |
| spelling it aloud: "T-R-A-P" | writing it on paper, miming it, pointing at the plaque |
| reading it aloud off a character sheet | the DM's own slips (DM rings the bell on themselves for fun; see coaching notes) |

**Each slip:** ring the bell, roll 1d8 on the table below, and the blast door **locks for 1 minute** (flip the sand timer; the book's 5-minute lockout is shortened so the station fits). A password spoken during a lockout is ignored. The ceiling keeps moving during a lockout unless Frank is braced.

## The Solution (DM only)
1.  **Halting the Ceiling (Frank):** All eight piston shafts meet at one stone gargoyle boss above the grate. Frank stands under it and uses **Massive Shoulders** (Action, automatic success) to hold the whole 4-ton ceiling as a living pillar. The tracker stops.
2.  **Cracking the Password (both):** Match the five ceiling words to their rack tags (by eye, or Investigation DC 12 per card). Read the first letters of the lit weapons from the entrance to the door: **S-N-A-R-E**. The archway plaque already lists "snares" as an allowed word, which is the hidden confirmation.
3.  **Opening the Door:** Either player speaks **"SNARE"** at the door (not during a lockout). The blast door grinds open.
4.  **Retrieving the Crest (Wayne):** The grate bars are only 4 inches apart (too small for weapons, crowbars or hands). Wayne listens and sniffs at the grate (*Wolf Senses*: Perception DC 10 with advantage) to hear the clink in the **right** pipe, then uses **Swarm of Pups** to send the puppies down it to haul the crest up by its chain. Sent without listening, roll 1d3: 1 left pipe (they bring back a broken sword hilt), 2 center pipe (they come back soaked with an old boot), 3 right pipe (crest!). Each wrong pipe costs about a minute.
5.  **The Exit:** Frank lets go and dives for the door: DC 10 Dex save or 3 (1d6) piercing on the way out. He always makes it.

## The 4 Progressive Clues & Handouts

### Clue 1: The Pulsing Words (Sensory / Environmental)
*   **Trigger:** Minute 2–3 if the pair still hasn't connected the ceiling words to the racks.
*   **Text (read aloud):**
    > *The ceiling grinds down another notch. The five glowing words on its underside pulse all at once, and each time they flash, a soft brass "ping" answers from somewhere along the weapon racks. Not all of the racks. Some of them.*
*   **Calibration:** Tells them the words and the racks are linked without saying which or how.

### Clue 2: The Keystone Boss (Character Sheet: Frank)
*   **Trigger:** Minute 5–6, when Frank takes initiative toward the ceiling (DC 11 Perception or Investigation, or automatic if he touches it).
*   **Text/Handout:**
    > *Frank notices the 4-ton spiked grid isn't one solid slab. All eight piston shafts meet at a single stone gargoyle boss right above the floor grate. Something big enough could hold that one point and stop the whole thing. Frank's shoulders twitch.*
*   **Calibration:** Points Frank at his sheet without naming *Massive Shoulders*.

### Clue 3: The Door's Mouth (Logic / Ordering Constraint)
*   **Trigger:** Minute 8–9, or earlier when a player inspects the door after matching at least 3 cards.
*   **Text/Handout:**
    > *Five empty sockets above the blast door light up, and the carved stone mouth whispers: "From the first steel to the last, take the head of every lit blade, and speak it plain."*
*   **Calibration:** Gives the rule (first letters, entrance-to-door order, say it aloud) but not the word.

### Clue 4: Bilge Surge (Emergency Fail-Forward, reserve only)
*   **Trigger:** Only after a critical mistake (e.g., the pups lost down the wrong pipe twice, or Frank never bracing and the ceiling at 5 ft) **or** at 11:00 if the pair is genuinely stuck. Use whichever variant fits.
*   **Variant A, stuck on the password:**
    > *The ceiling's weight cracks the five glowing words loose. They drop with a clang, each one landing on its own rack. The lit racks are now obvious.*
*   **Variant B, stuck on the crest:**
    > *A surge of drain water floods the sump! Wayne hears the brass chain sliding down the RIGHT pipe toward a bottomless drop. If anything small is going down there, it has to be NOW!*
*   **Calibration:** Bailout. It gives away a step but keeps the scene cinematic.

## Playtest Notes
*   **Simulated playtest (fixed version):** solved in **12m 10s** (target 10–15). Clues 1–3 rated Just Helpful Enough; Clue 4 not needed. Full turn-by-turn report: [Stations/Playtests/Station_5_Playtest.md](Playtests/Station_5_Playtest.md).
*   Key takeaways: expect 2–3 slips per pair (they pad the time and are the fun); the Elvish/Sylvan decoy costs about a minute and a half; a lucky Investigation roll can make the matching fast, so hold Clue 3 until 8:00 even with a quick group.

## Visual Props & Artifacts Specification (Ready for Generation)
*Use these prompt descriptions for an image-generation agent or graphic designer to generate the physical table assets. Items marked **[PLAYER HANDOUT]** go on the table; items marked **[DM ONLY]** stay behind the screen.*

### 1. The Armory Corridor Floor Plan & Ceiling Descent Tracker [PLAYER HANDOUT]
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 17" x 11" horizontal game mat or two-page spread.
*   **Art Style:** Top-down architectural battlemap of a 60-foot gothic stone armory corridor with eight weapon racks (four per side, numbered positions not shown), suit-of-armor sconces, a central iron floor grate, and a massive iron blast door with a carved stone mouth and five empty sockets above it.
*   **Rack Tag Slots:** a blank card-sized box beside each rack where the matching rack tag (prop 6) sits.
*   **The Ceiling Tracker Strip:** along the side, a vertical track with **16 notches, 15 FT down to 0 FT in 1-ft steps**; the 5 FT notch labeled **CROUCH** and the 0 FT notch labeled **CRUSH**. Use a paperclip slider or a clip-on token so it can't get bumped. Move it 1 notch every real minute.

### 2. The Drainage Grate & Sump Blueprint Handout [PLAYER HANDOUT, give when they examine the grate]
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 8.5" x 11" architect's cross-section blueprint.
*   **Art Style:** White-line architectural blueprint on deep blue paper, annotated with dwarven and gothic cursive notes.
*   **Visual Details:** Shows the central floor grate with an explicit **"4-INCH IRON BAR SPACING"** callout, leading down into three drainage branches labeled only **LEFT**, **CENTER** and **RIGHT** (no hint of which holds the crest; Wayne's senses decide that).

### 3. The Archway Plaque [PLAYER HANDOUT]
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 7" x 5" stone slab card.
*   **Art Style:** Cracked obsidian lintel stone carved with glowing red runes and a crossed-out skull symbol.
*   **Text:**
    > *"Speak not the hunter's word ('T---') within these walls, lest iron and fire answer your tongue. Walk in silence, or speak only of hazards, snares, cogs, devices, and mechanisms."*

### 4. The Eight d8 Penalty Flashcards [DM ONLY until rolled, then show]
*   **Dimensions/Format:** Set of 8 playing-card sized tokens (2.5" x 3.5"). Effects match the scaled penalty table above.
*   **Card 1 (Wall Arrows):** Slotted wall ports firing steel bolts (+5 to hit, 1d6+2 piercing).
*   **Card 2 (Constricting Vines):** Iron-spiked creeping vines bursting from stone joints (DC 13 Str save or Restrained).
*   **Card 3 (Heat Metal):** Red-hot glowing gauntlets dropping molten sparks (drop it or 1d8 fire).
*   **Card 4 (Blinding Flash):** A blinding burst of magnesium white glare (DC 13 Cha save or blinded 1 round).
*   **Card 5 (Flame Jet):** Roaring columns of orange fire venting from the walls (DC 13 Dex, 2d6 fire, half on save).
*   **Card 6 (Grease Siphon):** Jet of black slippery lard coating the flagstones (DC 13 Dex save or prone).
*   **Card 7 (Pendulum Blade):** Razor-sharp crescent blade swinging on an iron arm (+5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing).
*   **Card 8 (Ceiling Surge):** The spiked ceiling suddenly dropping 2 feet with a grinding crunch!

### 5. Standees & Tokens [PLAYER HANDOUT]
*   **Prop Token 1 (Swarm of Werewolf Pups):** Circular 2-inch token or standee depicting 8 chaotic, cute, red-eyed werewolf puppies gleefully tugging on a heavy brass chain.
*   **Prop Token 2 (Armory Crest #5):** A 3-inch circular metallic silver token depicting crossed battleaxes behind a spiked iron tower, with interlocking puzzle teeth along its rim.
*   **Extras:** a bell or squeaky toy, and a 1-minute sand timer for the door lockout.

### 6. Rack Tags & Ceiling Word Cards [PLAYER HANDOUT]
*   **Rack Tags (8):** brass-plate style cards (2.5" x 3.5"), each showing a **picture of the weapon** and its name written in one fantasy script (see the Room Setup table). The English name is printed small under the picture so players can read the first letter once a tag is lit. No numbers on the tags; their position on the mat gives the order.
*   **Ceiling Word Cards (5):** glowing-rune cards (2.5" x 3.5") in the same five scripts as the lit tags (Dwarvish, Goblin, Draconic, Elvish, Infernal), **no picture and no English**, dealt face up in a scrambled order. Each card's script must visibly match its tag's lettering. The Elvish card and the Sylvan (Halberd) tag share curly letters on purpose; the tell is that Elvish loops are **closed** and Sylvan loops are **open**. Make that difference clear at arm's length.
*   **Do not print any translations or rack numbers on the backs.**

## DM Tips & Running Recommendations
1.  **Bring a Bell / Squeaky Toy:** Whenever someone says the word, hit the bell with a grin and roll the d8. It turns a harsh penalty into a party game.
2.  **Reward Synonyms:** Praise them when they use silly alternatives like *"Watch out for that spicy floor mechanism!"* or *"The pointy ceiling contraption is moving!"*
3.  **End with a Dramatic Slide:** When the pups bring the crest and the door is open, let Frank make one final explosive shove upward and dive-roll under the blast door just as the ceiling crashes down!
