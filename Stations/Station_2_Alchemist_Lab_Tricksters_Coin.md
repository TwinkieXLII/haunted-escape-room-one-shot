# Station 2: The Alchemist's Laboratory — The Trickster's Coin

## Source & Metadata
*   **Source:** *The Game Master's Book of Traps, Puzzles and Dungeons* (Jeff Ashworth), Ch 12 Gateway Puzzles Part 2, from about p. 142 (exact page not recorded). *The Trickster's Coin*, reskinned from coins to alchemical vials. Book mechanics kept: find the one lighter counterfeit among 10 with a balance scale, place it in the bowl, Religion DC 10 reveals the trickster altar, numbered cardboard discs as props (book's GM tip). Changed: the book's wrong-coin penalty (smoke, lose 1d20 gp) is replaced by a harmless comedic side-effect table.
*   **Alternate:** *Wands and Keys* (Ch 11) is listed as the alternate in the overview, but Station 4 already uses it. If you need to swap this station out, pick a different Ch 11/12 puzzle.
*   **Estimated Playtime:** 10–15 minutes. Simulated playtest: **11m 20s** with the Trickster's Shuffle rule (Target Met). The fixed-answer version solved in **7m 40s** (too fast, solved by luck). Full report: [Station_2_Playtest.md](Playtests/Station_2_Playtest.md).
*   **Type:** Logic, deduction, and balance-scale weighing.
*   **Players:** Quasimodo (Artificer 5) & Shaggy Rogers (Rogue 5).
*   **Goal:** Find the single counterfeit (lighter) vial among 10 identical bottles using a balance scale, and offer it to the Trickster's Altar Bowl to unlock **Crest #2**.

> **Section visibility key:** sections marked **[DM ONLY]** never go on the table. Sections marked **[PLAYER HANDOUT]** are printed and handed over when earned. Everything else is DM reference.

## DM Notes (Coaching) [DM ONLY]

### What this room is testing
This is a reasoning room, not a perception room. Nothing about the vials looks, smells or feels different, so the players cannot spot the fake by inspection. They have to work out that a balance scale gives **three** answers (left rises, right rises, level), that weighing **groups** is faster than weighing pairs, and that a **level** result tells them about the vials they left **off** the scale. The skill checks only hand out flavour and clues. The scale is the puzzle. Your job is to answer the scale honestly and consistently and let them reason it out.

### How to describe the room (read aloud)
> *"The door creaks onto a cramped laboratory that smells of sulfur and burnt sugar. Ten amber bottles stand in a brass rack, each sealed with black wax and stamped with a number, one to ten. On the workbench sits a brass balance scale with two pans hanging from a grinning bat. Against the far wall, in front of a sealed iron door, a stone jester holds an empty brass bowl in its clawed hands and grins at you. There's a cluttered writing desk in the corner and a shelf of dusty ingredient jars."*

Then stop and ask: *"What do you do?"* Do not mention that one vial is fake. The altar inscription (Clue 1) tells them that.

### Running the scale (how to announce results)
*   Players place vial cards on the **LEFT PAN** and **RIGHT PAN** circles of the scale mat, then say "weigh."
*   Check the [Scale Response Rules](#how-the-scale-works-at-the-table-dm-only) and answer with **exactly one** of three set phrases, every time:
    *   **"The LEFT pan rises."** (put the tilt card on LEFT RISES)
    *   **"The RIGHT pan rises."** (tilt card on RIGHT RISES)
    *   **"The pans hang perfectly level."** (tilt card on LEVEL)
*   Always say which pan **rises** (the lighter side). Never switch to "drops" partway through. Mixing the two is the biggest cause of confusion at this table.
*   Tick off each weighing on the Weighing Log handout so everyone can see how many uses are left.

### Non-spoiler nudge lines (say these aloud; they are questions, never answers)
| Stage | What's happening | Nudge line |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Entry (0–3 min) | Wandering, poking at the jars | *"The jester's bowl looks like it's waiting for something. Anyone want a closer look?"* |
| After the inscription | Arguing about what "lightest" means | *"What in this room could tell you which thing is lightest?"* |
| First weighing | Weighing 1 vs 1 | *"Does the scale only take one bottle per pan?"* |
| Uneven pans | e.g. 3 vs 2 | *"How many vials are on each pan right now?"* |
| After a LEVEL result | "That told us nothing!" | *"Did it tell you nothing, or did it tell you something about the bottles on the table?"* |
| Stuck, many suspects | Going in circles | *"Which vials can you be sure are real right now?"* / *"How many suspects are left?"* |
| Near the end | 2–3 suspects, about to guess | *"Do you have a weighing left? Is guessing worth the risk?"* |

### What NOT to say or do
*   Don't say any vial's number as a hint, and don't look at, point to or touch the vial that's fake.
*   Don't say "you need 3 weighings" or "split them into groups of five." That is the puzzle.
*   Don't tell them a result is "useless." A level result always carries information.
*   Don't let the answer key, the solution trees or the suspect tracker sit where players can see them. Keep them behind the screen or in your lap.
*   Don't let skill checks replace the scale. No roll reveals the fake.

### Pacing checkpoints
| Clock | What you should see | What to do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0:00 | Read-aloud | Set out the rack (10 vial cards), the scale mat, the altar standee, the blank Weighing Log. |
| 2:00–3:00 | Players have found the altar | If not, use the entry nudge. Run **Clue 1** (inscription + Religion DC 10). |
| 5:00–6:00 | They are weighing or planning | If Quasimodo's player hasn't used Esmeralda yet, prompt: *"Quasimodo, your rat's whiskers are twitching at the rack."* That's **Clue 2**. |
| 8:00–9:00 | Deep in weighings, maybe stuck | Point them to the desk (Investigation DC 10) for **Clue 3**, the ledger. |
| 11:00 | Still stuck, OR right after a critical mistake | **Clue 4** (Shaggy's belch). Only now. |
| 14:00 | Not solved | Hard stop. Let the Trickster laugh and the door swing open ("He's bored of you"). No Crest penalty for a 14-year-old table; just move on. |

### Handling derails
*   **"I drink it!"** The black wax seals are enchanted and won't break. Only a drop leaks onto the drinker's tongue. Roll a d6 side effect for them. Shaggy takes no damage (Iron Stomach), and nobody else does either: it's comedy, not poison. The vial stays full, which keeps the weights honest. This is **not** a critical mistake.
*   **Weighing one vial at a time against nothing:** the loaded pan drops. *"Every bottle is heavier than an empty pan."* It counts as a use. Give the uneven-pans nudge.
*   **Weighing pairs one by one (1 v 2, 3 v 4...):** let them. Under the Trickster's Shuffle it is slow and will likely run past the 4-weighing creak. That's the lesson. Don't stop them.
*   **Hefting vials by hand:** the difference is a few grams, too fine for a hand to feel. *"They feel exactly the same."* No roll.
*   **Clever alternatives (the book says to reward them):** Quasimodo rigging a second scale from a ruler and two jar lids, floating a group of vials in a water basin to see which sits higher, Shaggy balancing a tray on one finger (Acrobatics), and so on. If the method is genuinely a comparison, treat it as **one free weighing** that doesn't count toward the cap, and answer it with the same Scale Response Rules. A good idea should feel good.
*   **Trying to put several vials in the bowl at once:** the jester spits them all back out. *"One offering."*
*   **Smashing a vial or the scale:** the glass is alchemically toughened. *"It bounces with a dull clonk."* Nothing breaks and no time penalty applies.

### Adjudicating the character hooks
*   **Quasimodo, "Esmeralda the Rat"** (sheet: *Action, send your pet rat to safely sniff out a trap or identify a poison*). Esmeralda finds the one bottle with a hairline crack in its seal and smells real quicksilver, so it is **genuine**. That's one suspect cleared. Default vial: **#1**. Under the Shuffle, if #1 is already cleared, she clears the lowest-numbered vial still a suspect. Use her only while 3 or more suspects remain. With 2 left she would hand them the answer, so she just squeaks and shrugs. She cannot find the fake: the counterfeit's seal is perfect.
*   **Shaggy, "Iron Stomach"** (sheet: *Bonus, eat food from your pockets to heal 1d4+4 HP*). In this room, (a) he takes no damage from tasting a vial, only the side effect; (b) he may spend a use to eat a snack and end a side effect on himself early; (c) it powers **Clue 4**, the bailout belch, which is held back until 11:00 or a critical mistake.
*   **Skill rolls:** neither sheet lists Religion or Investigation, so roll raw INT. Quasimodo rolls **d20 + 4**, Shaggy **d20 + 0**. Keep DCs at 10. Failing a roll only costs flavour and time, never the clue itself (see each clue).

### Keep the answer off the table
The Solution Logic, the answer key, the Suspect Tracker and the Clue 4 text are **DM eyes only**. Print the player handouts on separate sheets so you never hand over a page with the answer on the back.

## Room Setup
*   Ten amber glass vials sit in a brass rack, numbered **1 through 10**. All ten look, smell and feel identical. Nine hold genuine alchemical quicksilver. One is a **counterfeit**: same liquid, but blown with a hollow false bottom, so it weighs a few grams less. That's too little to feel by hand, but the scale shows it.
*   A two-pan brass balance scale sits on the main workbench.
*   A stone altar carved as a grinning jester, holding a brass bowl, stands before the sealed iron exit door.
*   A cluttered writing desk (drawer holds the ledger, Clue 3) and a shelf of dusty ingredient jars (decoration only; a good red herring for wandering players).

## How the Scale Works at the Table [DM ONLY]

**Default: the DM announces results (recommended).** Players move the cardboard vial cards onto the scale mat and the DM announces the result. No real weights are needed, and the cards are truly identical, so nobody can solve it by looking.

**Optional: real weights (Fixed mode only).** Use 10 identical opaque pill bottles or film canisters. Put the same number of coins in nine of them and one fewer coin in #6, then pad each with cotton so nothing rattles. Use a real kitchen balance and **test it before the session** (a cheap toy balance often can't detect one coin). Real weights only work with the fixed answer below, not with the Trickster's Shuffle.

### Scale Response Rules (apply in this order, every weighing)
1.  **Uneven counts** (for example 3 vs 2): the pan with **more** vials always drops and the other rises. The fake is only slightly lighter, so count beats counterfeit. This gives **no** information about the fake.
2.  **Even counts:** if the fake is on a pan, **that pan rises**. If the fake is on neither pan, the pans **hang level**.

### Which vial is the fake? Choose one mode before play.
*   **Trickster's Shuffle (recommended, added after playtesting).** The fake isn't fixed in advance. Keep the **Suspect Tracker** (10 boxes, DM only). On each even-count weighing, sort the current suspects into three groups: on the left pan, on the right pan, and off the scale. The fake **hides in the biggest group**. Announce the result that keeps that group as the suspects, and cross off everyone else. Ties: pick either group. Every answer you give stays consistent with one real fake, so the puzzle is always fair. But lucky guessing and pair-by-pair weighing no longer get rewarded, and good group weighing still solves in 3 (or 2). When one suspect remains, that vial is the fake. A vial offered to the bowl while two or more suspects remain is always "wrong" (cross it off).
*   **Fixed answer (book default): Vial #6 is the fake.** Simplest to run and required if you use real weights. Be aware: in the playtest, a pair weighing 1-v-2, 3-v-4, 5-v-6 found #6 on the third weighing by luck, about 7½ minutes in.

## Solution Logic [DM ONLY]

Both trees are general: letters stand for whichever vials the players pick. They show every branch, so they don't give away any particular answer. Use them to check whether the players' plan is efficient and how many weighings they have left.

### 10 suspects (no Esmeralda): 3 weighings guarantee it
Two weighings can only tell 3 × 3 = 9 cases apart, so 10 vials need 3. Three weighings cover up to 27, so 3 is always enough.

```
WEIGHING 1:  [A B C D E]  vs  [F G H I J]
                 |                  |
        LEFT rises            RIGHT rises         (Level is impossible: the fake
     fake in A-E           fake in F-J             must be on one pan.)
             \                /
         Take the lighter five; call them  p q r s t
                          |
WEIGHING 2:  [p q]  vs  [r s]      (t set aside)
           /            |              \
   LEFT rises       LEVEL            RIGHT rises
   p or q          t is FAKE         r or s
      |            (done in 2)          |
WEIGHING 3: p vs q                  r vs s
   the pan that RISES is the fake (it can't be level here)
```

### 9 suspects (Esmeralda clears one): 2 weighings, but only with a three-way split
```
WEIGHING 1:  [3 suspects]  vs  [3 suspects]     (3 set aside)
         /                 |                 \
  LEFT rises            LEVEL             RIGHT rises
  fake in left 3      fake in aside 3     fake in right 3
         \                 |                 /
WEIGHING 2:  [x]  vs  [y]     (z set aside)
   LEFT rises = x     RIGHT rises = y     LEVEL = z
```
A 4-vs-4 split (1 aside) with 9 suspects takes **3** weighings, not 2. Only the 3-3-3 split gets it down to 2.

### Fixed-mode answer key (Vial #6, Esmeralda clears #1)
*   10-vial run: W1 {1–5} vs {6–10}, RIGHT rises. W2 {6,7} vs {8,9}, 10 aside, LEFT rises. W3 6 vs 7, LEFT rises. **#6.**
*   9-vial run: W1 {2,3,4} vs {5,6,7}, {8,9,10} aside, RIGHT rises. W2 5 vs 6, 7 aside, RIGHT rises. **#6.**

### Weighing cap
Give **4 free weighings**. On the 4th, the bat fulcrum creaks ominously (a warning). From the 5th weighing on, the scale still answers, but the bat shrieks and the person who weighed rolls on the side-effect table. The puzzle never becomes unwinnable.

## Failure Consequences & Fun Penalty Table
**Critical mistake:** offering a wrong vial to the altar bowl. It produces an angry sizzle and a puff of colourful smoke, and the jester spits the vial back into the rack. (That vial is now known to be genuine: cross it off.) Instead of the book's gold loss, the person who offered it rolls 1d6 on the **Comedic Side Effects Table**. The same table is used for taste-testing and for weighings past the cap.

| d6 | Penalty Side Effect (lasts 5 real minutes or until the end of this station, whichever is first) |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Bubble Burps:** Player belches floating neon-pink bubbles every time they speak. |
| **2** | **Helium Voice:** Player's vocal cords tighten; they speak three octaves higher. |
| **3** | **Luminescent Glow:** Player's skin glows radioactive lime-green (disadvantage on Stealth). |
| **4** | **Sneezing Fit:** Violent comical sneezes that blow dust and paper scraps off the desks. |
| **5** | **Heavy Boots:** PC's feet feel like lead; speed reduced by 2 squares. |
| **6** | **Tingling Fingers:** Character drops small handheld items unless they hold them with two hands (the player must move vial cards with two fingers of *each* hand). |

No damage anywhere in this room. Shaggy may end a side effect on himself early with Iron Stomach.

## The 4 Progressive Clues & Handouts

### Clue 1: The Jester Altar Inscription (Exploration / Religion DC 10)
*   **Trigger:** Inspecting the carved altar bowl (aim for minute 2–3; use the entry nudge if they wander).
*   **Text / [PLAYER HANDOUT 1]** (anyone can read it; no roll needed):
    > *"The Trickster mocks honest gold and heavy metal. Give to my bowl the hollow lie, the weightless fraud, the lightest craft, and the gate shall grant you passage."*
*   **Religion DC 10 (Quasimodo d20+4, Shaggy d20+0):** *"This is an altar of the Smiling Shadow, god of forgers and frauds. His altars accept only the false thing, never the true one."* On a fail they keep the handout but don't learn the lore.
*   **Calibration:** Tells them the altar wants the **lightest** (fake) vial, not the heaviest or a mixture.

### Clue 2: Esmeralda's Nose (Artificer Hook)
*   **Trigger:** Quasimodo uses *Esmeralda the Rat* (an action) on the rack. Prompt at minute 5–6 if the player hasn't thought of it.
*   **Text (read aloud):**
    > *Esmeralda scurries along the rack, whiskers twitching. At Vial #1 she rears back and sneezes: there's a hairline crack in the black wax, and a thread of sharp, metallic quicksilver vapour leaks out. That one is the real thing. She pushes it out of line with her nose. The other seals are perfect, and she can't smell anything through them.*
*   **Effect:** Vial #1 is cleared (or the lowest-numbered suspect, under the Shuffle). That leaves 9 suspects, which can be solved in 2 weighings with a 3-3-3 split, or 3 otherwise. Only use it while 3 or more suspects remain.
*   **Calibration:** Rewards Quasimodo's sheet and shrinks the pool without doing the weighing for them.

### Clue 3: The Stained Alchemical Ledger (Logic Constraint)
*   **Trigger:** Searching the writing desk (Investigation DC 10, Quasimodo d20+4, Shaggy d20+0). Point them to the desk at minute 8–9. On a fail they still find it after a minute of rummaging (time cost only).
*   **Text / [PLAYER HANDOUT 2]:**
    > *"Night forty. My apprentice weighs the bottles two by two and wastes the whole night. Fool. A scale answers three ways, not two: left rises, right rises, or it hangs even. When it hangs even, it speaks of the bottles left on the table. Weigh many at once. Keep some aside. And never trust a scale with uneven pans."*
*   **Calibration:** Points out the three-outcome trick, the "set some aside" idea and the even-pans rule, without giving group sizes. Works for both 10 and 9 suspects.

### Clue 4: Shaggy's Iron Stomach Belch (Emergency Fail-Forward) [DM ONLY until triggered]
*   **Trigger (strict):** only **after a critical mistake** (a wrong vial offered to the bowl) when the table is also visibly stuck or frustrated, **or at 11:00** if they're still stuck. Keep it in reserve otherwise. A critical mistake makes it *available*, not mandatory.
*   **Text (read aloud):**
    > *Shaggy digs a squashed triple-decker sandwich out of his pocket and wolfs it down. Iron Stomach. A second later he lets out a concussive, echoing BURRRP. A stream of glowing pink bubbles drifts across the lab, sniffs around the rack like a lost puppy, and settles, fizzing, on top of three vials.*
*   **Effect:** The bubbles mark **three current suspects** (never just one). Fixed mode: three suspects including #6. Shuffle: any three suspects, which become the only suspects. If only 2 suspects remain, they mark both. The players still have to do the last weighing themselves.
*   **Calibration:** A deliberate bailout that saves the session clock but leaves the final deduction to the players.

## Visual Props & Artifacts Specification (Ready for Generation)
*Use these prompt descriptions for an image-generation agent or graphic designer to generate the physical table assets.*

### 1. The Ten Numbered Alchemical Vials
*   **Dimensions/Format:** Ten 2" x 3" vertical cards (or 2" cardboard discs, per the book's GM tip), one per bottle. All ten **must be printed from the same art** with only the number changed. The backs must be identical and blank (no answer markings).
*   **Art Style:** Renaissance apothecary glassware, dark amber ribbed glass, sealed with dripping black wax and brass wire, dense silvery quicksilver pooled at the base.
*   **Labels:** Large, high-contrast numerals stamped on the glass, readable from across the table: `VIAL 1` through `VIAL 10`. Use **Arabic numerals** (no Roman numerals) so 6 and 9 don't get confused; underline the 6 and the 9.
*   **No visual tell:** Vial 6 (or any vial) has nothing different about it: same liquid level, colour and meniscus. The weight difference exists only in the DM's rulings (or in the optional real-weight canisters).

### 2. The Two-Pan Balance Scale Mat
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 11" x 8.5" horizontal tabletop placemat.
*   **Art Style:** Top-down schematic of an ornate Barovian gothic brass balance scale.
*   **Visual Details:** Two large circular weighing pans labelled **"LEFT PAN"** and **"RIGHT PAN"**, each big enough to hold five 2" x 3" cards overlapping. They hang by brass chains from a central fulcrum shaped like a grinning bat. Below the fulcrum, a strip of three boxes, **LEFT RISES / LEVEL / RIGHT RISES**, where the DM sets the tilt card. Along the bottom edge, an **"Off the Scale"** tray area where set-aside vials go, so the three groups are always visibly separate.
*   **Tilt card:** A small brass-arrow token the DM slides into one of the three result boxes after each weighing.

### 3. The Trickster's Altar Card / Standee
*   **Dimensions/Format:** 5" x 7" vertical card or tabletop standee.
*   **Art Style:** Dark stone brazier carved in the likeness of a grinning jester with exaggerated fangs and hollow, soot-stained eyes. A deep brass offering bowl sits between its stone clawed hands. Label on base: *"Altar of the Smiling Shadow."* Players set the chosen vial card in front of the standee to offer it.

### 4. Handout Props
*   **Prop Handout 1 (The Altar Inscription) [PLAYER HANDOUT]:** Aged, singed parchment (8.5" x 5.5") with faded red ink:
    > *"The Trickster mocks honest gold and heavy metal. Give to my bowl the hollow lie, the weightless fraud, the lightest craft, and the gate shall grant you passage."*
*   **Prop Handout 2 (The Apothecary's Scorch-Marked Ledger) [PLAYER HANDOUT]:** A tattered notebook page with doodled scale sketches (decorative only: no numbers or group sizes drawn on the pans):
    > *"Night forty. My apprentice weighs the bottles two by two and wastes the whole night. Fool. A scale answers three ways, not two: left rises, right rises, or it hangs even. When it hangs even, it speaks of the bottles left on the table. Weigh many at once. Keep some aside. And never trust a scale with uneven pans."*
*   **Prop Handout 3 (Weighing Log) [PLAYER HANDOUT]:** A small card with five rows: `Weighing # | Left pan | Right pan | Result`. Row 4 is marked with a crack icon ("the scale creaks") and row 5 with a skull ("the bat shrieks"). Players fill it in with pencil.
*   **Prop Handout 4 (Comedic Penalty Cards):** Six small playing-card-sized tokens (Bubble Burps, Helium Voice, Radioactive Glow, Sneezing Fit, Heavy Boots, Tingling Fingers). Hand one to the affected player.
*   **Prop Handout 5 (Alchemist Crest #2):** A 3-inch circular metallic silver token showing a serpent coiled around a retort flask, with interlocking puzzle teeth on its outer rim.
*   **DM Prop (Suspect Tracker) [DM ONLY]:** A strip of ten numbered boxes behind the screen. Cross off cleared vials after each weighing (essential for the Trickster's Shuffle).

## DM Tips & Running Recommendations
1.  **Cap weighings at 4 (soft cap):** The 4th creaks, and the 5th and later cost a side-effect roll. This discourages slow one-by-one weighing without ever locking the puzzle.
2.  **Always announce which pan RISES:** *"The left pan rises, light as a feather."* Use the same three phrases every time and move the tilt card.
3.  **Lean into the side effects:** If a player triggers a penalty, really play up the bubble burps or helium voice. A table of 14-year-olds will laugh for two straight minutes and stay invested.
4.  **Track suspects on paper behind the screen:** under the Shuffle, a wrong ruling breaks the puzzle. Cross off boxes (including vials wrongly offered to the bowl) *before* you announce each result.
5.  **Esmeralda timing:** if the players start weighing pairs early, prompt Esmeralda around 4:00 so she clears a suspect they haven't already weighed.
