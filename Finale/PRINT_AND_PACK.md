# Finale — print and pack

Print `Finale_Complete_Print_Pack.pdf`: **12 Letter pages**, single-sided, Actual Size / 100%, color. This is a DM assembly file containing the culprit and confession.

| Complete pages | Use |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Lead-DM script: transition, opening the Gate, minion fight, accusation, capture, unmasking and ending. Keep behind the screen. |
| 5–6 | Grand Gate board halves: trim white margins and tape centre edges into a 15 × 10 inch board. Five 3-inch crest tokens fit in the five labelled circles. |
| 7 | Player-facing suspect board: show when the first pair returns. Keep whole or cut out the three suspect cards and instruction card. |
| 8 | Spare E1–E5: cut apart; use only to replace missing evidence. The original evidence travels in station Envelope 5. |
| 9 | Combat reference cards: cut apart for helpers; DM-only. |
| 10–12 | Twelve certificates (four per page): cut apart and hide until the unmasking. Each names the culprit. |

Set the Gate out before the opening; lay the suspect board face down nearby. Keep script, certificates and combat cards concealed. Each returning pair places its crest and evidence at the Gate. Give early arrivals the suspect board to discuss while other pairs finish. Save the final accusation until the whole group is together.

Add dice, pencils/pens, tape, scissors, timer and bell. No additional minion art or miniatures are required: use coins/tokens if desired. The gate is a labelled placement board; printed crests are not physically interlocking pieces.

The printable opening is separate: `Opening_Print_Kit.pdf`. Its editable story source is `Opening_Scene.md`, including the three staff introductions. This finale pack starts when pairs return from stations.

Digital page layout was visually checked; physical printer fit has not been tested. Do one sample print at 100% before cutting. Separate replacement PDFs: `Finale_DM_Script.pdf` (4 pages), `Grand_Gate_Board.pdf` (2), `Finale_Print_Kit.pdf` (6).

DM coaching: the current suspect descriptions make some individual clues sufficient to identify Anton. Let every pair present its evidence anyway; all five establish the full case. Do not announce the culprit before players accuse him. For Bruisers, use the stated two-hit table rule consistently; the HP number is a reference rather than a second tracking requirement.
