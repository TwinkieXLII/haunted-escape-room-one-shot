# Finale: Evidence & Suspects (source of truth)

**Culprit (chosen): Anton, the Alchemist's Apprentice.** He's the "fool" from Station 2's ledger, who weighed the bottles two by two and was fired for it. He rigged the Estate's hazards and plays the "Ghost of Count Von Zarovich" to scare off every visitor until he can claim the Estate's treasure for himself.

Each station's Reward envelope (Envelope 5) holds its Crest plus one Evidence Card. Every card matches one trait of the culprit. Each wrong suspect matches only one card, so they fail four.
