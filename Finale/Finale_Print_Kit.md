# The Suspect Board — Who Is the Phantom?

*Flip this over when the first pair reaches the Grand Gate. Players may read it.*

- **Hair:** Stained purple

- **Boots:** Short, small boots

- **Smells of:** Machine oil
<!-- /card -->

<!-- card: How to Solve It -->
Lay each **Evidence Card** you won beside the board. Every card describes **one thing about the Phantom**. Only one suspect matches **all five**.

When the Phantom hides behind the wolf door, say that suspect's name!
<!-- /card -->

# Spare Evidence Cards (if one goes missing)

<!-- card: E1 · The Borrowed Book (Library) -->
Tucked behind Barov's plinth: *Spooky Mechanisms for Beginners*, long overdue. The borrower's signature is smeared from right to left, the way a **left-handed** writer drags their hand through wet ink.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: E2 · The Singed Sleeve (Lab) -->
Under the workbench: a torn lab-coat sleeve peppered with tiny burn holes from splashed quicksilver. Whoever wore it **mixed potions every day**.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: E3 · The Oily Footprints (Observatory) -->
Gear-oil footprints lead behind the great clock: **small** boots, barely bigger than a kid's.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: E4 · The Mimic's Snack (Menagerie) -->
Stuck between the mimic's needle teeth: a tuft of hair stained bright **purple**.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: E5 · The Stinky Glove (Armory) -->
The pups drag up a glove along with the crest. It reeks of rotten eggs: **sulfur**.
<!-- /card -->

# Minion Cards (DM / helpers: one cluster each)

<!-- card: Minion Rule (read first) -->
- **Any hit destroys a minion.** No HP tracking.
- **Area powers** pop every minion they cover.
- **Bruisers** take 2 hits (12 HP).
- **Nobody goes down:** under 10 HP, minions steal hats and tie shoelaces instead of doing damage.
- **Each round:** every player acts once, then the minions act.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: Gear Gremlin · Vial Imp · Mini-Mimic -->
**AC 10 · 1 hit · Attack +3, 2 damage**
- Gear Gremlin: clanks, giggles, steals a gear.
- Vial Imp: spits acid, pops in purple bubbles.
- Mini-Mimic: a tiny treasure chest with teeth.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: Dancing Dagger -->
**AC 11 · 1 hit · Attack +3, 2 damage**
Pokes, twirls, clatters to the floor when hit.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: Clockwork Brute (Bruiser) -->
**AC 12 · 12 HP (about 2 hits) · Slam +4, 1d6+2**
Arrives in Wave 2. Ticks loudly. Great for Net Trap, Sandstorm Shuffle or a Look Into My Eyes! dance.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: Mimic Wardrobe (Bruiser) -->
**AC 12 · 12 HP (about 2 hits) · Bite +4, 1d6+2**
On a hit, the target is stuck inside until a friend uses an action to pull them out.
<!-- /card -->
<!-- card: Wave Plan -->
- **Round 1:** about one minion per player (mixed types).
- **Round 2:** both Bruisers + 4–6 minions.
- **Round 3:** mop up, then the Phantom flees.
- **Wrong accusation later:** 3 more minions, 1 round.
<!-- /card -->
