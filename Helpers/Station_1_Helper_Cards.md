# Station 1 Helper GM — The Library (Crown & Dagger)

**Pair:** Fred Jones (Fighter) & Griffin the Invisible Man (Rogue) · **Goal:** place the 6 relics in the right statues' hands → Archive Gate opens → **Crest #1 + Evidence Card E1**. **Target:** 10–15 min. Full rules: `Stations/Station_1_Library_Crown_and_Dagger.md`.

## Before the players arrive
- Six statue cards **first-name side up**: 1 Victoria · 2 Alexi · 3 Teresa · 4 Valerius · 5 Borislav · 6 Barov (on a box, he's on a tall plinth). Full titles stay face-down until the solve.
- Six relic tokens (book, scythe, dagger, spear, hammer, crown) **under a cloth "velvet runner"** on the reading table.
- Gargoyle card above the "fireplace"; Spectre standee hidden in your lap.
- **Telling Detail slips 1–6** in your hand, sorted. Envelopes 1–5 in order, Envelope 4 sealed. Phone timer.

## Your special job: the Telling Detail slips
- A player inspects a statue → **Investigation DC 13** (Fred +4, Griffin +5). Success: hand over that statue's slip and tuck it under the card.
- Fail: *"Only dust and stone. Try a different approach."* They may retry next turn with a new approach (touch, smell, candle, sleeve). The clue is never locked behind one bad roll.
- **Barov needs no roll.** Any close look (Mage Hand, a climb at Athletics DC 12, or a long stare after Envelope 3) finds *nothing, not even dust* → hand over slip 6.

## Station clock
| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Read the Player Introduction aloud. Start the timer. |
| 2:00–3:00 | **Envelope 1** when they lift the runner (no roll) or inspect the table (DC 11). Not found by 3:00? The runner slides off by itself. |

# Station 1 Helper GM — Running the Room

## Failure rules
- **Placement only:** handling/inspection is safe. An offering to a statue counts; moving a token around the table does not.
- **Any wrong placement:** the statue shudders and *every* relic placed so far clatters back to the table. Sweep them all back yourself, loudly.
- **2nd wrong placement onward:** the gargoyle shrieks at everyone it can see: DC 12 Wis save or 1d6 psychic. **Griffin is invisible and never targeted.** Point that out the first time; he'll love it.
- **Crown ↔ Dagger swap** (dagger to Barov or crown to Teresa): a minor **Spectre** appears (AC 12, 14 HP, +4 to hit, 1d6 necrotic). Fred's Net Trap (DC 13 Dex) plus Griffin's Sneak Attack ends it in about a minute. This unlocks Envelope 4 early.
- **Taboo warning:** before any crown or dagger placement, say *"The gargoyle's eyes flare red. Are you sure?"* Say it **every time, right or wrong**, so it gives nothing away.

## Nudges (questions only, never answers)
| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Nobody touches the table (2–3 min) | *"That velvet runner is lumpy. Anyone curious?"* |
| Guessing, not looking (3–5 min) | *"These are statues of real people. What would a statue remember about the person?"* |
| Hooks unused (5–6 min) | Fred: *"Your sheet says you love splitting up."* Griffin: *"Barov's hands are way up there. Anything on your sheet that reaches far?"* |
| Five stains found (8–9 min) | *"What's strange about the sixth statue?"* / *"Have you checked the plinths?"* |
| Arguing over the rhyme | Give it a minute, then: *"What does a tiller do in a field?"* |

## Character rulings
- **Fred, "Let's Split Up!"** (station ruling): once here, Fred and his partner inspect two different statues at once, **both with Advantage**.
- **Griffin, "Mage Hand Shenanigans":** inspects Barov's plinth without climbing and can float the crown into Barov's raised hands.
- Accept Fred's **Survival** (mud) or Griffin's **Sleight of Hand** (blood under nails) as inspection rolls on the fitting statues.

## Don't
- Don't name an item-statue pair or confirm a pairing before the token is placed. "Try it and see" is fine.
- Don't point at Barov or call him spotless before Envelope 3.
- Don't read the full statue titles aloud or let players see this card.
- Don't open Envelope 4 early because the table is noisy.

## Reward (Envelope 5)
Flip the statue cards to show the full titles, then hand over **Crest #1** and **Evidence Card E1**. Say: *"Take these to the Grand Gate. Hurry!"*
