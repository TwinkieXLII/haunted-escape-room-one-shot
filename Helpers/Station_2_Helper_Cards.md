# Station 2 Helper GM — The Alchemist's Lab (Trickster's Coin)

**Pair:** Quasimodo (Artificer) & Shaggy Rogers (Rogue) · **Goal:** find the one **lighter** counterfeit vial among 10 using the balance scale and offer it to the jester's bowl → **Crest #2 + Evidence Card E2**. **Target:** 10–15 min. Full rules: `Stations/Station_2_Alchemist_Lab_Tricksters_Coin.md`.

## Before the players arrive
- Ten **identical** vial cards in the rack, in order 1–10. Scale mat with the tilt card on **LEVEL**. Jester altar standee.
- Weighing Log and a pencil on the table. Side-effect cards (d6) in your hand. Envelope 3 (the ledger) "in the desk drawer".
- **Pick a mode with the lead GM:** ☐ **Fixed:** vial **#6** is the fake (simplest) · ☐ **Trickster's Shuffle:** keep the Suspect Tracker (boxes 1–10) on your clipboard.

## Your special job: running the scale
Players put vial cards on the LEFT and RIGHT pans and say "weigh." Answer with **exactly one** of these, every time, and move the tilt card:
> "The LEFT pan rises." · "The RIGHT pan rises." · "The pans hang perfectly level."
- **Uneven counts** (e.g. 3 vs 2): the pan with **more** vials drops, so the other pan rises. This tells them nothing.
- **Even counts:** the pan holding the fake **rises**. If the fake is on neither pan → **level**.
- Always say which pan **rises**. Never switch to "drops" partway through.
- **Shuffle mode:** before answering, split the current suspects into *left / right / off the scale*. The fake hides in the **biggest** group. Announce the result that fits, then cross off everyone else.
- **Weighing cap:** 4 free weighings. On the 4th the scale **creaks**. On the 5th and later the bat **shrieks** and whoever weighed rolls a side effect. It never becomes unwinnable.

## Station clock
| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Read the Player Introduction aloud. Start the timer. |
| 2:00–3:00 | **Envelope 1** when they inspect the altar. Lore: Religion DC 10 (Quasimodo d20+4, Shaggy d20+0). |
| 4:00–6:00 | **Envelope 2** when Quasimodo sends Esmeralda. Prompt if needed: *"Quasimodo, your rat's whiskers are twitching at the rack."* |
| 8:00–9:00 | Point them at the desk: **Envelope 3** (Investigation DC 10; on a fail they still find it after a minute). |
| 11:00 | Still stuck, or after a wrong offering with a frustrated table: **Envelope 4**. |
| 15:00 | Lead GM's bell = hard stop. Finish fast, then **Envelope 5** and send them to the Gate. |

## Answer key (Fixed mode, fake = #6)
- 10 vials: W1 {1–5} v {6–10} → RIGHT rises · W2 {6,7} v {8,9} (10 aside) → LEFT rises · W3 6 v 7 → LEFT rises → **#6**.
- 9 vials (Esmeralda cleared #1): W1 {2,3,4} v {5,6,7} → RIGHT rises · W2 5 v 6 (7 aside) → RIGHT rises → **#6**.

# Station 2 Helper GM — Running the Room

## Failure rules (no damage anywhere in this room)
- **Wrong vial offered to the bowl:** angry sizzle, colourful smoke, the jester spits it back (it's now **cleared**). The person who offered it rolls d6:

| d6 | Side effect (lasts 5 min or until the station ends) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bubble Burps: pink bubbles every time they speak |
| 2 | Helium Voice |
| 3 | Radioactive Glow (disadvantage on Stealth) |
| 4 | Sneezing Fit |
| 5 | Heavy Boots (speed −2 squares) |
| 6 | Tingling Fingers (move cards with two fingers of each hand) |

## Quick rulings
- **"I drink it!"** The wax seals are enchanted. Only a drop gets through: roll a side effect, no damage. The vial stays full. Not a critical mistake.
- **Hefting vials by hand:** *"They feel exactly the same."* No roll.
- **One vial against an empty pan:** the loaded pan drops. It counts as a weighing.
- **Several vials in the bowl:** spat back out. *"One offering."*
- **Clever ideas** (a homemade second scale, floating vials in water): count it as **one free weighing** and answer with the same rules.

## Nudges (questions only)
| When | Say |
|---|---|
| Wandering | *"The jester's bowl looks like it's waiting for something."* |
| Weighing 1 vs 1 | *"Does the scale only take one bottle per pan?"* |
| After LEVEL: "that told us nothing!" | *"Did it tell you nothing, or something about the bottles left on the table?"* |
| Going in circles | *"Which vials can you be sure are real right now?"* |
| About to guess | *"Do you have a weighing left? Is guessing worth the risk?"* |

## Character rulings
- **Quasimodo, "Esmeralda the Rat":** clears **one** genuine vial (#1, or the lowest-numbered suspect in Shuffle mode). Use only while 3 or more suspects remain. With 2 left she just squeaks and shrugs. She can never find the fake.
- **Shaggy, "Iron Stomach":** no harm from tasting. May spend a use to end his own side effect early. Powers Envelope 4.

## Don't
- Don't say a vial number as a hint, or look at the fake.
- Don't say "use 3 weighings" or "split into fives." That *is* the puzzle.
- Don't call a level result useless. Don't let a skill roll replace the scale.

## Reward (Envelope 5)
Hand over **Crest #2** and **Evidence Card E2**: *"Take these to the Grand Gate!"*
