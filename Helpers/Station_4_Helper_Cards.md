# Station 4 Helper GM — The Mimic Menagerie (Wands & Keys)

**Pair:** Scooby-Doo (Barbarian) & Murray Mummy (Druid) · **Goal:** test four identical keys with four wands and lift the real one with Mage Hand → **Crest #4 + Evidence Card E4**. **Target:** 10–15 min. Full rules: `Stations/Station_4_Mimic_Menagerie_Wands_and_Keys.md`.

## Before the players arrive
- Four **identical** alcove cards, Alcove I–IV. Four wand cards; a **purple bead on Poison** and a **green bead on Acid** (one charge each).
- Reveal tiles (Illusion Dispelled, Mimic, Disarmed Construct) face down in your box. The Menagerie plaque (Envelope 3) face down under the wand rack.
- Shuffle Tracker on your clipboard with a **paperclip on the starting layout**. **A real snack for Scooby; check allergies.** Phone timer.

## Your special job: the shuffle and the wands
- **Collect the bead before narrating.** The moment a player fires Poison or Acid, hold out your hand for the bead.
- **Read the matrix wording exactly.** Identical outcomes get identical words, so plain results are clues too.
- **A wrong key lifted** (by hand, mage hand, whip, or a thrown object) triggers that alcove, then **reset**: read the reset line, move the paperclip **A → B → C → D → A**, clear all reveal tiles, return both beads.
> Reset line: "With a grinding roar, the four arches spin like a roulette wheel and lock back into place. Whatever you learned, it's scrambled now. Two fresh gems click into the wand cradles."

| Content | Bright Light | Poison (1 charge) | Acid (1 charge) | Mage Hand (lifts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Illusion** | Shadow stays frozen. **Reveal.** | "The mist drifts straight *through* the key." **Reveal.** | "The acid passes through and hisses on the wall behind." **Reveal.** | Gong, cold vapour. **Reset.** |
| **Mimic** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | Gags and slumps into a grey blob. **Reveal.** | "The acid splashes over the key and hisses on the pedestal. The key looks unharmed." | Bites, lunges. **Reset.** |
| **Construct** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | "The mist beads on the metal and drifts away." | Casing melts, fuse dies. **Reveal (disarmed).** | Explodes. **Reset.** |
| **Real key** | "Its shadow swings with the beam." | "The mist beads on the metal and drifts away." | "The acid splashes over the key and hisses on the pedestal. The key looks unharmed." | **Success!** |

# Station 4 Helper GM — Running the Room

## Station clock
| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Read the Player Introduction aloud. Start the timer. |
| 2:00–3:00 | **Envelope 1** when they look around, or someone reaches for a key with no plan. |
| 5:00–6:00 | **Envelope 2**: toss Scooby a real snack if he hasn't used Snack Surge. |
| 8:00–9:00 | **Envelope 3** on a wand-rack search (Investigation DC 10), or reveal it at 9:00. |
| 11:00 | Stuck, or a reset after 10:00: **Envelope 4**. |
| 13:00 | Not solved: the real key starts to glow faintly. |
| 15:00 | Lead GM's bell = hard stop. Then **Envelope 5** and send them to the Gate. |

## Hazards (scaled for teens)
- **Mimic** (AC 12, 15 HP): one bite at whoever disturbed it, +5 to hit, **1d8+2 piercing + 2 acid** (it crunches a mage hand instead and gets no bite). Then Murray's Shuffle and Scooby's bites finish it, then **reset**.
- **Construct:** everyone within 30 ft makes a **DC 12 Dex save, 2d4** damage, half on a save. Then **reset**.
- **Illusion:** a gong, no damage. **Reset.**

## Character rulings
- **Scooby, Scooby Snack Surge** (needs a real snack): +14 Perception vs DC 12. Scooby asks **one** question: *"alive?"* (finds the mimic) or *"fake?"* (finds the illusion). After a reset, only DC 15 without a surge, unless you give a mercy snack.
- **Murray, Sandstorm Shuffle** (Action): automatically restrains the mimic for 2 rounds.
- **Murray, Nature DC 10:** *"Mimics digest with acid. Acid won't bother one, but poison makes them gag."*
- **Murray, Bandage Whip** (reach 2 squares): lifting a key counts as Mage Hand. **Tapping a pedestal** (not the key) is safe and gives that alcove's Envelope 4 sound.

## Nudges (questions only)
- About to grab a key: *"Does anything here tell you what happened to the last people?"*
- Zapping at random: *"What question does each wand answer?"*
- Forgot the charges: *"Anything printed on the wand cards you haven't used?"*
- Ignoring Scooby: *"Scooby, your stomach just growled."*
- Two keys left, no charges: *"Anything either of you can do that isn't a wand?"*

## Don't
- Don't hint at any alcove, and keep a straight face when they point at the real one.
- Don't put a reveal tile or the mimic standee out until it's earned.
- Don't call them "the mimic wand" or "the bomb wand"; use the wand names.

## Reward (Envelope 5)
Hand over **Crest #4** and **Evidence Card E4**: *"Take these to the Grand Gate!"*
