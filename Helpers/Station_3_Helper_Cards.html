Station 3 Helper GM — The Clockwork Observatory (Gears & Gadgets)

Pair: Count Dracula / Drac (Bard) & Johnny (Bard) · Goal: load four gears on the vault door pegs in the right order and turn the crank → the Phantom is locked away → Crest #3 + Evidence Card E3. Target: 10–15 min. Full rules: `Stations/Station_3_Clockwork_Observatory_Gears_and_Gadgets.md`.

Before the players arrive

  • Vault door board (PEG 1–4, crank in the centre, small ↑ above Peg 1). Seven identical brass gears in a jumbled pile (pencil marks on the backs are for you).
  • Clockwork Phantom standee. Plaque card (hand over when anyone looks at the door). Star chart (hand over when anyone touches the astrolabe or desk).
  • Phone timer, plus a second alarm for the Phantom's tick (3:00, then every 2 minutes).

Your special job: the Phantom's tick

  • Strikes at 3:00, 5:00, 7:00, 9:00, 11:00... and immediately after any wrong crank. One line of narration, one roll, back to the puzzle. No initiative.
  • Scythe Sweep: +5 to hit, 1d6+2 slashing (Drac AC 14, Johnny AC 13). Alternate targets.
  • If a target is under 10 HP: the Phantom knocks one placed gear back into the pile instead of dealing damage.
  • Phantom: AC 15, 22 HP, Wis save +0. At 0 HP it bursts into gears, skips its next strike, then reassembles. It can't be destroyed; only the door locks it away.
  • The crank: a correct set opens the door. A wrong set grinds and shrieks, gives no hint about which gear was wrong, and the Phantom strikes at once.

Station 3 Helper GM — Running the Room

Character rulings (from the sheets; 3 uses each)

  • Drac, Look Into My Eyes! (Action): the Phantom saves DC 15 Wis at +0. On a fail it skips its next strike. From 11:00, a success also triggers Envelope 4.
  • Drac, Bat Form (Bonus): flies to the dome → Envelope 2.
  • Drac, Inspiring Bleh (Bonus): a d8 for Johnny's next roll or save.
  • Johnny, DJ Backpack (Reaction when the Phantom attacks him): the attack still lands, but the Phantom skips its next strike.
  • Johnny, Sick Beats (once at this station): after a wrong crank, cancels that crank's strike and pops the gears back out with no penalty.
  • Johnny, Chill Vibes / Monster Mash: a d8 for any roll, or 1d8 healing. Use freely.

Quick rulings

  • Attacking the Phantom: allowed and fun, but it reassembles. After a round, say: "The pieces are already crawling back together. The door locks it away."
  • Interrogating it (Persuasion or Intimidation +7 vs DC 15): it ticks once per tooth on one peg. Give them Peg 1.
  • They miscount the Dragon (6 instead of 7): don't correct them. Ask "Does that match what the riddle says about Two and Three?"
  • Prying the door: it's magical and won't budge. Redirect kindly.

Don't

  • Never say a tooth or star count before Envelope 4. Never say "stars equal teeth" before Envelope 3.
  • Never confirm a single gear. Only a full crank gives feedback.
  • Don't flip the gears over, and don't let the Phantom take more than about 30 seconds of any 2 minutes.

Envelope Contents

Envelope 1 · The Painted Dome (read aloud)
The clock ticks and, high above, the painted dome flickers. For a heartbeat the constellations on the ceiling glow, exactly matching the brass star chart on the desk, and a thin golden line runs from the chart's arrow down to the little arrow above Peg 1.
Envelope 2 · The Gilded Stars (read aloud)

Bat Form:

Up close, only three of the six painted constellations are inlaid with real celestial brass: the little Bat right under the arrow, the long coiled Dragon, and the curved Scythe. The Raven, the Coffin and the Wolf are just dull grey paint.

Johnny, History DC 12:

Old Barovian stargazers called the Bat "the first wing," the Dragon "the great wyrm," and the Scythe "the reaper's blade."
Envelope 3 · The Crank Inscription (read aloud)
Worn letters are stamped around the crank's hub: STAR FOR TOOTH · TWO AND THREE TURN AS ONE · ALL TOGETHER: THE SKY'S HEART.

Optional: after any wrong crank, Johnny may roll Performance DC 13 to hear how many gears rang true, but not which ones.

Envelope 4 · The Hypnotized Clockface (read aloud)
The Phantom's clockface spins wildly and its ticking slows into words, the old clockmaker's rhyme: "Four in one... and three in four..." Then it freezes.

That gives Pegs 1 and 4. The twins rule plus the 21 total gives 7 + 7. At 13:00 the frozen Phantom points at each correct gear.

Envelope 5 · Evidence Card E3: The Oily Footprints

Goes in with Crest #3.

Gear-oil footprints lead behind the great clock: small boots, barely bigger than a kid's.
