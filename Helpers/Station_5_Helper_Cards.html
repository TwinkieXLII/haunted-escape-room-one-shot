Station 5 Helper GM — The Collapsing Armory (Taboo Word)

Pair: Frankenstein / Frank (Barbarian) & Wayne Werewolf (Barbarian) · Goal: stop the ceiling, decode the door password, retrieve the crest from the grate, without saying the forbidden word → Crest #5 + Evidence Card E5. Target: 10–15 min. Full rules: `Stations/Station_5_Collapsing_Armory_Corridor_of_the_Taboo_Word.md`.

Before the players arrive

  • Corridor mat (Sheets A + B taped together). Rack tags in their slots: A-top-1 Spear · A-bottom-1 Mace · A-top-2 Net · A-bottom-2 Axe · B-top-1 Glaive · B-bottom-1 Rapier · B-top-2 Halberd · B-bottom-2 Executioner's Blade.
  • Five ceiling word cards face up, scrambled. Tracker slider at 15 FT. Archway plaque ready to hand over. Blueprint (give it when they examine the grate).
  • Bell (or squeaky toy), 1-minute sand timer by the door, penalty cards 1–8 face down, a d8, the pups token. Phone stopwatch.

Your special job: the word, the bell and the ceiling

  • The forbidden word is TRAP. You never say it, not even in rules talk. Call it "the word" and point at the plaque.
  • Counts: trap, traps, trapped, trapdoor, booby-trap, mousetrap, "it's a trap!", spelling it aloud, reading it off a sheet. Doesn't count: strap, trapeze, other languages (piège, Falle, trampa), writing, miming, pointing.
  • Each slip: ring the bell → roll d8 and show that penalty card → flip the sand timer (the door is locked 1 minute, and passwords during a lockout are ignored). The ceiling keeps moving.
  • Watch Frank's sheet: the Massive Shoulders text contains the word. If Frank's player reads it aloud word for word, ring the bell with a big grin.
  • The ceiling: every real minute, slide the tracker down 1 ft and say it aloud ("Thirteen feet. Grrrind."). CROUCH at 5 ft (half speed). CRUSH at 0 ft (minute 15): the floor tilts and dumps everyone out the door (2d6 each, nobody drops below 1 HP). It stops the moment Frank braces.

Station 5 Helper GM — Running the Room

Answer key (DM eyes only)

  • Ceiling: Frank stands under the gargoyle boss and uses Massive Shoulders (Action, automatic success). While braced he can't move, but his player can still talk and sort cards.
  • Password: match the 5 ceiling words to rack tags (by eye, or Investigation DC 12 per card). Lit racks: Spear, Net, Axe, Rapier, Executioner's Blade. First letters from entrance to door = SNARE. The plaque already lists "snares" as an allowed word. Wrong passwords just make the stone mouth cough dust.
  • Decoy: the Sylvan Halberd tag looks like the Elvish Rapier card. Elvish loops are closed; Sylvan loops are open.
  • The crest is in the RIGHT pipe. Wayne's Wolf Senses: Perception DC 10 with advantage hears it. Swarm of Pups fetches it, and one use lasts the whole station. Sent without listening, roll 1d3: 1 left (broken sword hilt), 2 centre (soggy boot), 3 right (crest!). Each wrong pipe costs about a minute.
  • Exit: Frank lets go and dives for the door: DC 10 Dex or 1d6 piercing. He always makes it.

Nudges (questions only)

  • "The words on the ceiling are glowing. How many racks are there? How many words?"
  • "Do any of those squiggles look like anything else in the room?"
  • "Who in the party has the loudest muscles for that ceiling?" (point at Frank, not at the ability)
  • "You've got five weapons. What's the door waiting for?" / "Read your plaque again. What are you allowed to talk about?"
  • At the grate: "Your hands won't fit. Who's small enough?" / "Wayne, which way do your nose and ears point?"

Derails

  • Saying it on purpose for laughs: it still counts. On a third deliberate slip within a minute, it's automatically result 8: "the room gets bored of the joke."
  • Arguing over variants: one sentence from the table above, then move on.
  • Clever workarounds (other languages, miming, a hand over a mouth): allowed. Praise them!
  • Prying the grate or door: fused to the bedrock. Give them a fair roll, but it doesn't budge.

Don't

  • Never say the word, "SNARE", "first letters" or "in order" before Envelope 3. Never point at a rack.
  • Never name Massive Shoulders or Swarm of Pups; point at the sheets instead. Nobody goes to 0 HP here.

Envelope Contents

Envelope 1 · The Pulsing Words (read aloud)
The ceiling grinds down another notch. The five glowing words on its underside pulse all at once, and each time they flash, a soft brass "ping" answers from somewhere along the weapon racks. Not all of the racks. Some of them.
Envelope 2 · The Keystone Boss (read aloud)
Frank notices the spiked ceiling isn't one solid slab. All eight piston shafts meet at a single stone gargoyle boss right above the floor grate. Something big enough could hold that one point and stop the whole thing. Frank's shoulders twitch.
Envelope 3 · The Door's Mouth (read aloud)
Five empty sockets above the blast door light up, and the carved stone mouth whispers: "From the first steel to the last, take the head of every lit blade, and speak it plain."
Envelope 4 · Bilge Surge (read aloud)

A · stuck on the password:

The ceiling's weight cracks the five glowing words loose. They drop with a clang, each one landing on its own rack. The lit racks are now obvious.

B · stuck on the crest:

A surge of drain water floods the sump! Wayne hears the brass chain sliding down the RIGHT pipe toward a bottomless drop. If anything small is going down there, it has to be NOW!
Envelope 5 · Evidence Card E5: The Stinky Glove

Goes in with Crest #5.

The pups drag up a glove along with the crest. It reeks of rotten eggs: sulfur.
