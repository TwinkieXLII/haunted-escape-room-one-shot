# Station 2 — print and pack

Use **Station_2_Complete_Print_Pack.pdf** for one complete set (13 Letter pages). Print single-sided, at **Actual Size / 100%**, with color enabled. Do not additionally print the component packs unless you need replacements.

| Complete pack pages | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Player introduction: give at entry. |
| 2–3 | Scale halves: trim white margins and tape adjoining centre edges; finished image is 15 × 10 inches. |
| 4 | Ten identical-back vial cards (about 0.8 × 1.33 inches) and off-scale tray. Cut along white gutters. |
| 5 | Weighing log, scale-result strip and cut-out marker. Start marker on LEVEL. Add a pencil. |
| 6 | Jester altar: cut out and mount in a clip or cardstock base. |
| 7 | Two parchment clues: cut apart. Top in Envelope 1; bottom in Envelope 3. |
| 8 | Six numbered side-effect cards: cut apart; helper holds hidden. |
| 9 | 3-inch crest: cut out and put in Envelope 5. |
| 10–11 | Helper rules: helper-only sleeve. Choose Fixed #6 or Shuffle before play. |
| 12 | Cut out five envelope labels and contents. Use illustrated clues from page 7 instead of duplicate text cards for Envelopes 1 and 3. Put E2 in Envelope 5 with the crest. |
| 13 | DM suspect tracker: behind the screen, essential for Shuffle. |

Envelope 2 contains Esmeralda's character prompt; Envelope 4 contains the emergency clue and remains helper-only until triggered. Never hand players the intact helper cut-out page. The complete PDF contains answers, so it is a DM assembly file.

Physical supplies: five small clue/reward envelopes, a larger station packet, pencil, scissors, tape, cardstock/standee clip, d6, stopwatch and helper screen. No actual weights or real balance are required; the helper announces scale results.

The 15-inch board is sized for the small cards to fit five abreast across each pan's centre. PDF layouts and dimensions were checked digitally; do one actual-size sample print to verify your printer setting before cutting the batch.

Separate PDFs are available for replacement printing: Player Print Pack (5 pages), Reveals Print Pack (3 pages), DM Print Pack (4 pages). The existing introduction is also available separately. Rebuild with `build_print_packs.py` using Python with reportlab and pypdf installed.
