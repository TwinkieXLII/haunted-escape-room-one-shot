# Station 1 — complete print kit

Print `Station_1_Complete_Print_Pack.pdf`: **16 Letter pages**, single-sided, Actual Size / 100%, color. It includes DM answers: assemble before handing out individual props.

| Pages | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Player introduction. |
| 2–7 | Six ancestor statue cards. First names only; visible at entry. |
| 8 | Six stone relics and gargoyle watcher; cut apart. |
| 9 | Six telling-detail slips: helper holds until inspected. |
| 10 | Reading-table inscription and plinth plate: cut apart; staged discoveries. |
| 11 | Crest, Spectre and full-title labels: keep hidden. Crest goes in Envelope 5; show title labels only after solve. |
| 12–13 | DM answer and coaching notes: helper-only sleeve. |
| 14–15 | Helper running sheets and envelope schedule. |
| 16 | Cut-out envelope labels and clue/evidence contents. |

Prepare five small envelopes:
1. Reading-table inscription (use illustrated card from page 10 instead of duplicate text).
2. Character hook prompt from page 16 (helper reads aloud).
3. Plinth plate (use illustrated card from page 10 instead of duplicate text).
4. Emergency whisper from page 16, helper-only until triggered.
5. Crest from page 11 **and E1 from page 16**.

Keep detail slips, title labels, Spectre and all helper pages concealed. This set supplies separate full-title labels; when the helper says to flip statue cards after the solve, show the labels instead. Do not glue them onto fronts. Handling relics is safe; only incorrect placement triggers consequences. Spectre: dagger to Barov or crown to Teresa.

Add: cloth runner, small box/book to raise Barov, standee clips/cardstock feet, scissors, pencil, tape, dice, stopwatch, character sheets. Print a sample to verify dark details and sizes before cutting. Digital layouts were visually checked; physical printing has not been tested.
