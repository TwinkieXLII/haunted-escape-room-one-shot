## Print packs
- [Player starting props](Station_1_Player_Print_Pack.html): six 4 × 6 inch ancestor cards, six relic tokens and gargoyle watcher.
- [DM-held discoveries and reveals](Station_1_Reveals_Print_Pack.html): six 3.5 × 2 inch inspection slips, reading-table inscription, plinth plate, Archive Crest #1, Spectre standee and full-title labels.
- [DM notes](Station_1_DM_Notes.html): answer table, inspections, character hooks, failure triggers, coaching and emergency hints.

Print on Letter at 100%, background graphics ON, browser headers/footers OFF. Single-sided. All sheets have 0.5-inch margins. Cut out props separately from instructions.

Keep telling-detail slips hidden until inspected; no stains or professions appear on starting statue cards. Reveal full titles only after solving. Raise Barov's card on a small box or book. The current station's explicit failure rules apply: placing the wrong relic triggers consequences, not simply picking one up.

The reading-table clue prints at 7.2 × 4.8 inches to fit standard margins. Plinth is 5 × 3 inches, crest 3 inches, Spectre 2 inches tall; relics approximately 2.4 inches square. Statue images are 256 ppi at 4 × 6; relic sheet and reading clue approximately 209–213 ppi. Remaining small props exceed 300 ppi. Print a sample to check dark details. Crest teeth are decorative, not a physically fitted lock.
