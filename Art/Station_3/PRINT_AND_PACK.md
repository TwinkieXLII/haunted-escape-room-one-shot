# Station 3 — print and pack

Print `Station_3_Complete_Print_Pack.pdf`: **14 Letter pages**, single-sided, Actual Size / 100%. The complete file contains DM answers and is an assembly copy; give players only the intended props.

| Complete pages | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Player introduction. |
| 2–3 | Vault door halves: trim white margins and tape centre edges together, yielding a 15 × 10 inch mat. |
| 4–5 | Seven brass gears: cut around the toothed silhouettes, keep backs blank, mix together. Include every gear from both pages. |
| 6 | Current riddle plaque: cut out; give when they look at the door. |
| 7–8 | Astrolabe chart halves: trim margins and tape centre edges. Full chart is 10 inches square; optional circular trim. Give on astrolabe/desk inspection. |
| 9 | Clockwork Phantom standee: cut out and mount in clip/cardstock foot; visible at entry. |
| 10 | Observatory Crest #3: cut out and put in Envelope 5. |
| 11–12 | Helper running rules: keep behind the screen. |
| 13 | Cut-out envelope labels/contents: clues 1–4 are read aloud by helper at their triggers; E3 goes in Envelope 5 with crest. |
| 14 | DM assembly/validation checklist. |

Prepare five small envelopes and a separate helper sleeve. Never hand players the intact clue sheet or answer key. No correct-gear labels, colored decoys or solution mappings appear on starting props. The crank inscription is staged in Envelope 3; it is not visible on the door at entry.

Supplies: scissors, tape, cardstock/standee clip, dice, pencil, helper screen, stopwatch and a separate Phantom tick alarm. First strike at 3:00, then every 2 minutes and after a wrong crank.

Do one sample print at 100% before batch cutting. Digital checks confirmed all seven gear outlines (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 7, 8), both matching sevens, constellation counts (Bat 4 / Coffin 5 / Dragon 7 / Raven 6 / Wolf 8 / Scythe 3), XXI, and page fit. Physical printer output has not been tested.

Separate replacement packs: Player 8 pages, Reveals 1 page, DM 4 pages. The complete pack includes the existing one-page introduction. Do not print both complete and separate packs unless you want duplicates.
