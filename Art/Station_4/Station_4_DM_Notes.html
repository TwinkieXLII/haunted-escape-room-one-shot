Hazards

DM ONLY — Coaching & clue timing

Start neutrally

Four identical silver wolf-crest keys wait under arches. Four wands rest on a table. Describe each key the same way. Do not expose the reveal cards, plaque or crest.

2–3 minutes: orientation

“What happened to the last people here?” Describe scorches, tooth gouges and sticky residue beyond the brass studs, spread across all four alcoves. Point out the two single charge sockets.

5–6 minutes: character hook

“Scooby, anything on your sheet that a snack helps with?” Roll d20 +14 vs DC 17 (1–2 fail). On success: alive identifies the current mimic; fake identifies the current illusion. On failure: surge spent, no location or false clue, no reset; continue with wands or Murray’s lore. Neither identifies the real key. Use the current layout.

8–9 minutes: logic clue

Reveal the plaque after the rack search, or when dust is smeared away. Do not assign its verses to alcove numbers.

11 minutes, or reset after 10: emergency

Prompt Murray to tap with his bandage whip. Mimic: wet snore. Construct: ticking. Illusion: passes through key. Real: hollow brass bell. Give locations from the current layout. Accept a player’s clever early tap.

13–15 minutes

At 13 minutes the real key glows if needed. Finish by 15 minutes with a funny cost. Do not stall an early solve.

Keep tests fair

Use the reaction table’s identical words for identical outcomes. A plain result still conveys evidence. Collect Poison/Acid tokens before resolving the test. A reset clears every previous observation.
