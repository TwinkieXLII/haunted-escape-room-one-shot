# Station 4 — complete print kit

Print `Station_4_Complete_Print_Pack.pdf`: **16 Letter pages**, single-sided, Actual Size / 100%, color. It includes DM answers: assemble before handing out individual props.

| Pages | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Player introduction. |
| 2–5 | Four identical alcove cards labelled I–IV; visible at entry. |
| 6–7 | Four diagnostic wand cards; visible at entry. |
| 8 | Two charge tokens, brass-stud boundary marker and investigation log. |
| 9 | Hidden reveal tiles and crest; cut apart. Crest goes in Envelope 5. |
| 10 | Hidden Menagerie plaque; cut out for Envelope 3. |
| 11–12 | DM reaction matrix, layouts, coaching and current rules. |
| 13–14 | Helper running sheets and envelope schedule. |
| 15 | Cut-out envelope labels and clue contents 1–4. |
| 16 | E4 evidence card: put in Envelope 5 with crest. |

Prepare five small envelopes:
1. Scorched-floor prompt from page 15 (helper reads aloud).
2. Scooby prompt from page 15 (roll before reading successful result).
3. Illustrated plaque from page 10 instead of duplicate text version.
4. Emergency bandage-tap prompt from page 15, helper-only until triggered.
5. Crest from page 9 **and E4 from page 16**. Award E4 on completion even if the mimic was never tested; narrate that a loose tuft catches the light as the alcoves open.

Keep layout tables, reveals and helper sheets concealed. Put Poison and Acid charge tokens on their matching wands. Choose a starting layout; move to the next after resets. Acid-disarmed construct is harmless to lift; its scrap cannot unlock the door and does not reset the room. Reset rearms it. Scooby: d20 +14 vs DC 17; 1–2 fail, surge spent, no location or false clue, no reset.

Add: real safe snack, pencil, paperclip to track layout, standee clip/cardstock feet, scissors, dice, stopwatch, character sheets. Optional purple/green beads replace the paper charge tokens. Print a sample before cutting. Digital layouts were visually checked; physical printing has not been tested.
